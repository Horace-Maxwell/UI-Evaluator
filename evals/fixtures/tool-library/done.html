    What happens next

    Come to the Fernhill community centre, 14 Mill Lane, during your session. Bring your membership card and the deposit in cash or by card. A volunteer will check the tool with you and note the return date.

    If you cannot make the session, call the desk on 01632 960552 during opening hours and we will hold the tool for the next one.
