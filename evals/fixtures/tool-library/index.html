    Opening times

    The library is open on Saturdays from 10:00 to 13:00 and on Wednesdays from 18:00 to 20:00, at the Fernhill community centre, 14 Mill Lane. Reservations are collected and returned during those hours.

    Fernhill Tool Library is run by volunteers. Questions: call the desk on 01632 960552 during opening hours.
