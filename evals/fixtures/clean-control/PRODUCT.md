# Hollin Lane Seed Library: product context

Read: for library members who grow food and flowers at home; the main job is borrowing seed packets for the season; tone referent is the hand-written seed-desk ledger the volunteers keep. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]

Contents: Product summary · Users and groups · Top tasks · Context of use · Positioning · Constraints · Brand commitments · Facts · Principles · Accessibility needs · Platforms and locales · Target assurance level · Surfaces

## Product summary

A community seed library run from the Hollin Lane branch of the public library. Members browse the seed catalogue online and request up to three varieties, which volunteers pack and keep at the seed desk. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]
Unlike a shop, nothing is sold: members bring back some of the seed they save, so next season's catalogue grows from local gardens. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]
