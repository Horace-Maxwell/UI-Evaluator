How lending works

  1. Choose up to three varieties and request them online or at the seed desk.
  2. Collect your packets within 14 days. Each packet holds enough seed for a small bed or a few pots.
  3. Save seed from your best plants and bring some back by 30 November, so the next grower can borrow it.

The seed desk is open on Saturdays from 10:00 to 13:00 at Hollin Lane Library.

Questions about borrowing: call 0161 496 0731.
