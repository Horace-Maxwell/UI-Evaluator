Opening days

The centre is open every day except Wednesday, 08:00 to 18:00, at Quarry Road, DN4 2XQ. Slots last 20 minutes; the last slot starts at 17:20. A household can book two slots a week.

Bring the reference and something with your address on it. Vans and cars with trailers need a free permit.
