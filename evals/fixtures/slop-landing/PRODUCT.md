# Partbook: product context

Read: for volunteer choir directors and choir secretaries; the main job is planning a term of rehearsals and who sings what; tone referent is a well-kept rehearsal register, warm and orderly. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]

Contents: Product summary · Users and groups · Top tasks · Context of use · Positioning · Constraints · Brand commitments · Facts · Principles · Accessibility needs · Platforms and locales · Target assurance level · Surfaces

## Product summary

Partbook is a web tool for amateur and school choirs: directors plan a term of rehearsals, assign parts and music to each section, and see who is coming before each rehearsal. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]
Unlike a general calendar, it knows about voice parts, so one change to the plan reaches every singer in the affected section. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]
