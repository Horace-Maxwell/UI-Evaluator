# Linchuan Ceramics Museum audio guide: product context

Read: for Chinese-speaking visitors in the galleries; the main job is reading or hearing about the object in front of them; tone referent is a knowledgeable docent speaking quietly beside the case. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]

Contents: Product summary · Users and groups · Top tasks · Context of use · Positioning · Constraints · Brand commitments · Facts · Principles · Accessibility needs · Platforms and locales · Target assurance level · Surfaces

## Product summary

The text version of the audio guide at the Linchuan Ceramics Museum (临川陶瓷博物馆), a small regional museum of Song and Yuan ceramics. Visitors type the number on a display-case label and read, or listen to, a short commentary about that object. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]
Unlike the rented audio handsets, it runs on the visitor's own phone and its text can be enlarged. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]

## Top tasks

1. Open the commentary for an exhibit by its case number. Critical; journey `find-exhibit-by-number`. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]
2. Read an exhibit's commentary comfortably on a phone. Core. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]
3. Browse the exhibits of a gallery. Peripheral. [inferred: front-desk visitor notes]

## Context of use

- Devices: visitors' own phones, held in one hand. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]
- Scene: standing in dim galleries in front of a display case; quiet, so many visitors read rather than listen. [inferred: front-desk visitor notes]
- Frequency: several exhibits per visit, a few visits a year. [inferred: ticket office records]
- Connectivity: museum wifi, weak in the lower gallery. [inferred: front-desk visitor notes]
- Assistive technology: larger system text sizes are common; some visitors use screen readers (VoiceOver, TalkBack). [inferred: front-desk visitor notes]

## Accessibility needs

- WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor; China's GB/T 37668 web accessibility standard is the local reference. [inferred: museum digital policy]
- Large system text and zoom are common among older visitors. [inferred: front-desk visitor notes]
- Test with: VoiceOver on iOS Safari in Chinese, TalkBack on Android in Chinese, a pinyin input method, 200% zoom. [inferred: museum digital policy]

## Platforms and locales

- Platform profile: web. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]
- Browsers: Safari on iOS, Chrome and the WeChat in-app browser on Android. [inferred: museum website analytics]
- Locales: zh-CN, Simplified Chinese, left to right; no Traditional Chinese edition. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]
- zh-CN form of address: 您, because this is a public cultural service and many visitors are older; never mixed with 你. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]
- Formats: dates as 2026-10-01 or 2026年10月1日; 24-hour times; lengths in 厘米. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]

## Target assurance level

Target level: L3. A public museum service used by older visitors and screen-reader users; the needs-human checks (screen readers in Chinese, input methods) must be done by a person. [confirmed: fixture brief, 2026-10-01]
