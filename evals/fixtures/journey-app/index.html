Book a visitor berth

Visiting boats can stay on our pontoons for up to seven nights. Boats come in and go out over the sill gate, so plan your arrival around high water.

    Harbour rules

    • Keep to 4 knots inside the harbour walls.
    • Raft alongside another boat only when the harbour office asks you to.
    • Boats over 12 metres moor at the wall berth.

    Harbour office: call 01632 960418, open 08:00 to 20:00 every day.
