## How to use

- Use each term exactly as defined here, with one name per concept ([AUTHORING §2](AUTHORING.md#2-voice), [§3](AUTHORING.md#3-ids-and-naming)). When a file needs a term that is missing, add the entry here first instead of coining a synonym.
- The 中文 column is the preferred translation for Chinese documents such as `README.zh-CN.md` and `docs/OVERVIEW.zh-CN.md`. Renderings marked （建议译名） are this project's choice where Chinese HCI and design writing has no settled term; unmarked renderings follow established usage or are plain literal translations.
- Identifiers stay in English in every language: gate and criterion IDs, enum values such as `not_run` or `single_location`, mode and strategy names in config and `DESIGN.md`, file names and commands. In Chinese prose, write the Chinese term and give the identifier on first use, for example 操作模式（Operate）.
- A concept with named values (levels, gates, states, problem types, modes, strategies) is one entry, with its values listed in the same row.
- Definitions summarise. If an entry and its linked section disagree, the section wins and the entry is a bug. Code-font paths such as `methods/…`, `knowledge/…` and `workflows/…` are under `skills/ui-evaluator/references/`; `assets/data/…` is under `skills/ui-evaluator/`.
- Fixed renderings: gate → 关卡 (never 门槛, which reads as 阈值 "threshold"); finding → 发现项 (never 问题, and never "issue" in English); criterion → 准则, but a WCAG success criterion → 成功标准; severity labels → 外观问题 / 次要问题 / 重大问题 / 灾难性问题.
- 中文说明：本表是术语名称、含义与中文译名的规范来源。中文文档一律采用“中文”列的译名；标注（建议译名）者为本项目暂定译法。ID、状态值、模式名、文件名与命令保留英文原文。

## 1. Framework and gates

框架与关卡

| Term | 中文 | Definition | Defined in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Principle** (P1–P14) | 原则 | One of fourteen constitutional rules that every rule, workflow and prompt traces to; in a conflict, P11 accessibility outranks P12 honesty, then P1 context and brief, P9 user evidence, P3 convention and P2/P4 taste. | [FRAMEWORK §3](FRAMEWORK.md#3-principles) |
| **Workflow**, lifecycle | 工作流；生命周期 | One of nine procedures (setup, direct, build, audit, study, ingest, fix, verify, report) that together form the lifecycle, a loop that alternates analytical evaluation (audit) with empirical evaluation (study, ingest). | [FRAMEWORK §5](FRAMEWORK.md#5-the-lifecycle); `workflows/<name>.md` |
| **Depth** | 评估深度：快速 `quick`、标准 `standard`、严格 `rigorous` | How much an audit runs: `quick` is one labelled single pass with provisional severities and reaches at most L1, `standard` (the default) adds isolated evaluators, walkthroughs, critics and three blind raters and can reach L2, and `rigorous` adds more evaluators across model configurations, baseline comparison and a human-confirmation pass and can reach L3. | [FRAMEWORK §7](FRAMEWORK.md#7-evaluation-depth) |
| **Input situation** | 输入情形 | What the skill was given (source and a running app, a live URL, screenshots or design exports, a brief, a harness without subagents, missing or failing tools), which decides what can run and what may be claimed; screenshots alone reach no assurance level. | [FRAMEWORK §14](FRAMEWORK.md#14-input-situations-and-degraded-operation); [ADR-023](DECISIONS.md#adr-023--static-only-inputs-cannot-reach-an-assurance-level) |
| **Assurance level** | 保证等级：L1 机器验证、L2 评审团审查（建议译名）、L3 人工确认、L4 用户验证 | The strongest verifiable quality claim a run supports: L1 Machine-verified (every deterministic check passes), L2 Panel-reviewed (L1 plus G5 and G6), L3 Human-confirmed (L2 plus a named human's confirmations and the needs-human WCAG checks) or L4 User-validated (L3 plus G7); reports give the target level, the achieved level and what blocks each unmet level. | [FRAMEWORK §2.3](FRAMEWORK.md#23-the-promise-stated-so-it-can-be-checked); [QUALITY-BAR §3](QUALITY-BAR.md#3-assurance-levels) |
| **Gate** (G0–G7) | 关卡：G0 证据完整性、G1 功能完整性、G2 无障碍、G3 工艺底线、G4 有意设计与去 AI 味、G5 分析式可用性、G6 设计质量评审团、G7 实证验证（G3–G6 为建议译名） | One of eight quality checkpoints (G0 evidence integrity, G1 functional integrity, G2 accessibility to WCAG 2.2 AA, G3 craft floor, G4 deliberateness and anti-slop, G5 analytical usability, G6 design-quality panel, G7 empirical validation), each a set of criteria whose worst state becomes the gate's state. | [FRAMEWORK §4.6](FRAMEWORK.md#46-gates-and-assurance-levels); [QUALITY-BAR §4](QUALITY-BAR.md#4-the-gates) |
| **Criterion** | 准则 | A checkable requirement with an ID of the form `<DOMAIN>-<NN>` (such as `TYP-03`, never renumbered), a threshold, a verification method and a source; inside a finding, the criterion is whatever the problem violates: a heuristic, a CW question, a WCAG success criterion or a rule ID. | [QUALITY-BAR §1](QUALITY-BAR.md#1-purpose); [AUTHORING §3](AUTHORING.md#3-ids-and-naming); [FRAMEWORK §4.3](FRAMEWORK.md#43-findings) |
| **Criterion state**, gate state | 准则状态、关卡状态：通过 `pass`、未通过 `fail`、未运行 `not_run`、不适用 `not_applicable`、降级 `degraded`、已豁免 `waived` | The outcome recorded for each criterion; a gate takes the worst of its criteria in the order fail > not_run > degraded > waived > pass, any state without artifacts is `not_run`, and `not_run` never counts as a pass. | [QUALITY-BAR §2.1](QUALITY-BAR.md#21-criterion-and-gate-states), [§2.2](QUALITY-BAR.md#22-evidence-requirements) |
| **Waiver** | 豁免 | The owner's recorded acceptance of a failed G3–G6 criterion, stating who decided, why and the scope (one value by default, criterion-wide only with explicit consent), kept in `.ui-evaluator/waivers.json` and listed in every report; G0–G2 failures can never be waived, and threshold overrides in `config.json` likewise need a reason and can never lower a WCAG threshold. | [QUALITY-BAR §2.3](QUALITY-BAR.md#23-waivers-and-accepted-tells) |
| **[calibrating]** | 校准中 | The tag on a threshold or detector rule that is an expert proposal not yet measured on labelled data; it gates as written, but the owner may overrule a failure with a recorded reason until calibration settles the value through an ADR. | [QUALITY-BAR §7](QUALITY-BAR.md#7-calibration-status-of-thresholds); [EVALUATION-PLAN §7](EVALUATION-PLAN.md#7-calibration-programme) |
| **Surface** | 界面类型（建议译名） | A screen or page type, such as a landing page, settings, a dashboard, an article or an onboarding step, each of which declares exactly one mode. | [FRAMEWORK §4.1](FRAMEWORK.md#41-surfaces-and-modes) |
| **Mode** | 模式：说服 Persuade、操作 Operate、阅读 Read、体验 Experience（建议译名） | What the visitor is doing on a surface, declared per surface rather than per product, which sets expression, the default colour strategy, the motion budget and some thresholds: Persuade (deciding whether to care; high brand expression and one signature moment), Operate (doing repeated work; low expression, Restrained colour, feedback and state motion only, frequent actions ≤ 150 ms), Read (reading or learning; a typographic voice and Restrained colour) or Experience (immersive pieces; the most expression, with budgets declared per piece). | [FRAMEWORK §4.1](FRAMEWORK.md#41-surfaces-and-modes); [QUALITY-BAR §5](QUALITY-BAR.md#5-mode-dependent-thresholds); [ADR-022](DECISIONS.md#adr-022--modes-are-declared-per-surface) |
| **Productive / expressive** | 生产型 / 表现型（建议译名） | IBM Carbon's two style registers, onto which the modes map: Operate and Read surfaces are productive, Persuade and Experience surfaces expressive. | [FRAMEWORK §4.1](FRAMEWORK.md#41-surfaces-and-modes) |
| **`PRODUCT.md`** | 产品上下文文件 | The context file at the project root holding users and groups, top tasks, context of use, positioning, constraints, brand commitments, the facts section, principles, accessibility needs, platforms, locales and the target level, but never visual decisions. | [FRAMEWORK §4.2](FRAMEWORK.md#42-context-artifacts) |
| **Facts section** | 事实清单（建议译名） | The part of `PRODUCT.md` that lists every claim the UI may make (metrics, customers, prices) together with known absences; proof shown in the UI but missing from it counts as fabricated (SLP-12). | [FRAMEWORK §4.2](FRAMEWORK.md#42-context-artifacts); [QUALITY-BAR G4](QUALITY-BAR.md#g4--deliberateness-and-anti-slop) |
| **`DESIGN.md`**, decisions log | 设计规范文件；决策日志 | The design file at the project root in Google's design.md format (YAML tokens plus fixed prose sections), extended with modes per surface, "X, not Y" brand attributes, colour strategy, motion tokens and budget, the direction contract, accepted tells and a decisions log of dated decisions with reasons; for an existing product it is written from the shipped build. | [FRAMEWORK §4.2](FRAMEWORK.md#42-context-artifacts), [§4.7](FRAMEWORK.md#47-ledgers-and-registers) |
| **Journey** (critical user journey) | 用户旅程（关键用户旅程） | A JSON record of persona, goal, a scenario written without UI words, start state, an observable success condition, the correct action sequence and criticality, replayed by `uie journey` for FUN-03 and walked in cognitive walkthroughs. | [FRAMEWORK §4.2](FRAMEWORK.md#42-context-artifacts); [ARCHITECTURE §8.2](ARCHITECTURE.md#82-schemas) |

## 2. Findings and severity

发现项与严重程度

| Term | 中文 | Definition | Defined in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Finding** | 发现项 | One problem recorded once as a schema-valid record, never as chat prose, holding what and where, the violated criterion and why it harms the user, evidence, ratings and a separate, advisory recommendation; English text says "finding", not "issue". | [FRAMEWORK §4.3](FRAMEWORK.md#43-findings); [ARCHITECTURE §8.3](ARCHITECTURE.md#83-the-finding-record) |
| **Candidate finding** | 候选发现项 | A finding as first raised by an evaluator, critic, auditor or code reviewer, or by user evidence such as a feedback theme or an analytics anomaly, before the verifier confirms or rejects it; it carries no severity yet. | [FRAMEWORK §8.2](FRAMEWORK.md#82-the-finding-state-machine), [§9.2](FRAMEWORK.md#92-ingesting-feedback) |
| **Problem type** | 问题类型：单一位置 `single_location`、多处位置 `multiple_locations`、整体结构 `overall_structure`、元素缺失 `missing_element` | The CMU lecture's four kinds of problem: confined to one place, the same problem at several places (one record listing every location), a flaw in the structure of the whole, or something users need that is absent, which must be backed by positive evidence of absence. | [FRAMEWORK §4.3](FRAMEWORK.md#43-findings), [§8.1](FRAMEWORK.md#81-writing-rules) |
| **Severity** (0–4) | 严重程度：0 不构成问题、1 外观问题、2 次要问题、3 重大问题、4 灾难性问题 | Nielsen's scale as used in the lecture (0 not a problem, 1 cosmetic, 2 minor, 3 major, 4 catastrophe), rated after consolidation by at least three raters blind to one another and to any suggested severity, with the mean of the problem and trade-off values as the final value (a not-a-problem vote carries no value, ADR-029); quick depth gives a provisional single-rater value, and deterministic findings take their rule's declared default. | [FRAMEWORK §4.5](FRAMEWORK.md#45-severity-priority-ease-of-fix-criticality); [METHODS §5](METHODS.md#5-consolidation-verification-and-rating); [ADR-010](DECISIONS.md#adr-010--deterministic-findings-carry-rule-declared-severity) |
| **Severity factors** | 严重程度因子：频率、影响、持续性、业务影响 | What each rater scores before choosing the 0–4 label (frequency 0–3, impact 0–3, persistence 0–2, optional business impact), from which a monotone suggested severity, impact + ⌊(frequency + persistence) / 2⌋ capped at 4, serves as a prior the rater may depart from with a note. | [METHODS §5](METHODS.md#5-consolidation-verification-and-rating) |
| **Divergent finding** | 分歧发现项（建议译名） | A finding whose raters' problem and trade-off values spread by 2 or more (highest minus lowest), or that has a "not a problem" vote against a mean of 2.5 or more; it keeps its mean for ordering but becomes `disputed` and is settled by user research, never by a vote. | [FRAMEWORK §4.5](FRAMEWORK.md#45-severity-priority-ease-of-fix-criticality); [ADR-018](DECISIONS.md#adr-018--disagreement-goes-to-users-not-to-a-vote); [ADR-029](DECISIONS.md#adr-029--validity-votes-stay-outside-the-severity-mean) |
| **Priority** (P0–P3) | 优先级 | The fix order derived from mean severity (≥ 3.5 P0, ≥ 2.5 P1, ≥ 1.5 P2, ≥ 0.5 P3, otherwise dropped), raised to at least P1 for G1 and G2 failures and capped at P2 for findings that affect only peripheral journeys. | [FRAMEWORK §4.5](FRAMEWORK.md#45-severity-priority-ease-of-fix-criticality); [QUALITY-BAR §6](QUALITY-BAR.md#6-priority-policy-for-gating) |
| **Ease of fix** (1–4) | 修复难易度 | A separate estimate by the white-box role, as the lecture assigns it to the development team: 1 one value or token, 2 one component or file, 3 several components or one flow, 4 information architecture or architecture (a higher number is harder). | [FRAMEWORK §4.5](FRAMEWORK.md#45-severity-priority-ease-of-fix-criticality) |
| **Criticality** | 关键度（建议译名）：关键 `critical`、核心 `core`、外围 `peripheral` | A journey's importance, which caps the priority of findings that affect only that journey. | [FRAMEWORK §4.5](FRAMEWORK.md#45-severity-priority-ease-of-fix-criticality) |
| **Finding status** | 发现项状态：候选、已确认、已拒绝、待处理、处理中、等待决策、已修复、已验证、已解决、已延期、有争议、不予修复、已驳回、已重开、已过时、已拆分 | A finding's place in the state machine (`candidate`, `confirmed`, `rejected`, `open`, `in_progress`, `blocked`, `fixed`, `verified`, `resolved`, `deferred`, `disputed`, `wont_fix`, `dismissed`, `reopened`, `stale`, `split`), where `stale` means the UI changed under its anchor and it must be re-checked before it is reported, `blocked` means it waits on a named person's decision and still counts as open (ADR-036), and `split` means it was replaced by its parts (ADR-037). | [FRAMEWORK §8.2](FRAMEWORK.md#82-the-finding-state-machine) |
| **Verified vs resolved** | 已验证 vs 已解决 | A fixed finding becomes `verified` only on evidence its fixer did not produce: a fresh fix reviewer's `confirmed_fixed` for a judged finding, a re-run of the same check that no longer reports a deterministic finding, or a human's confirmation; the fixer's own re-test reaches only `fixed`. Only a human, or the original reporter of a feedback-sourced finding, may move it to `resolved`. | [FRAMEWORK §8.2](FRAMEWORK.md#82-the-finding-state-machine); [ADR-020](DECISIONS.md#adr-020--humans-close-feedback-sourced-findings); [ADR-030](DECISIONS.md#adr-030--verified-needs-independent-evidence) |
| **Dismissal ledger** | 驳回台账（建议译名） | `.ui-evaluator/dismissals.json`, the findings a human rejected as not a problem, which the verifier keeps from being raised again under another heuristic unless new evidence exists. | [FRAMEWORK §4.7](FRAMEWORK.md#47-ledgers-and-registers) |
| **Debt register** | 债务登记簿（建议译名） | `.ui-evaluator/debt.md`, the deferred findings, each with a reason, an owner and a revisit trigger, including the P2 and P3 items left out of the "fix now" queue of P0, P1 and quick P2 wins. | [FRAMEWORK §4.7](FRAMEWORK.md#47-ledgers-and-registers); [QUALITY-BAR §6](QUALITY-BAR.md#6-priority-policy-for-gating) |
| **Fix contract** | 修复契约（建议译名） | The acceptance criterion written before a fix touches code: the check that raised the finding plus the regression checks the fix must also pass. | [FRAMEWORK §10](FRAMEWORK.md#10-fix-discipline) |
| **Regression** | 回归 | Anything a fix breaks (a newly introduced deterministic finding by identity, an unexplained pixel change outside the target, lost ARIA names, roles or landmarks, or console errors absent from the baseline), which makes the fix revert. | [METHODS §8](METHODS.md#8-fix-verification); [FRAMEWORK §10](FRAMEWORK.md#10-fix-discipline) |
| **Agree/disagree sheet** | 认同/异议表（建议译名） | The sheet `uie report` produces for the owner or a named expert to confirm or overrule findings and the G6 verdict; completing it is part of reaching L3. | [ARCHITECTURE §7.2](ARCHITECTURE.md#72-commands); [QUALITY-BAR §3](QUALITY-BAR.md#3-assurance-levels) |

## 3. Evidence

证据

| Term | 中文 | Definition | Defined in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Evidence level** (E0–E5) | 证据等级：E0 预测、E1 已复现、E2 专家确认、E3 已观察、E4 用户实测、E5 因果确证 | How strongly a finding or claim is supported, which sets the wording it may use: E0 one unverified pass ("may"), E1 measured by a tool, reproduced by a separate verifier or found by two independent passes, E2 confirmed by a human expert, E3 seen with real users ("k of n participants"), E4 quantified with users with a confidence interval, E5 established by a randomised experiment (only then causal verbs). | [FRAMEWORK §4.4](FRAMEWORK.md#44-evidence-levels); [ADR-008](DECISIONS.md#adr-008--evidence-levels-govern-wording) |
| **Evidence anchor** | 证据锚点（建议译名） | The pointer that ties a finding to the rendered UI (route, state, selector or bounding box, screenshot crop, reproduction steps, optionally file:line) and that must resolve in the captured state (EVD-05). | [FRAMEWORK §4.3](FRAMEWORK.md#43-findings); [QUALITY-BAR G0](QUALITY-BAR.md#g0--evidence-integrity) |
| **Fingerprint** | 指纹 | A finding's identity, a hash that leaves out line numbers to avoid phantom regressions: criterion, location and a normalised snippet for a judged finding; rule ID and normalised route for a deterministic finding, however many instances (locations) it lists. It is used to compare runs as sets (cleared, introduced, persisting, with a persisting deterministic finding reported as k of n instances cleared) and to mark findings `stale` when the UI changes (EVD-08). | [FRAMEWORK §4.3](FRAMEWORK.md#43-findings); [ARCHITECTURE §8.3](ARCHITECTURE.md#83-the-finding-record); [ADR-033](DECISIONS.md#adr-033--identity-of-grouped-tool-findings) |
| **Absence rule** | 缺失举证规则（建议译名） | A claim that something is missing must come with positive evidence of absence (the states searched and the controls inventoried), or it is not reported. | [FRAMEWORK §8.1](FRAMEWORK.md#81-writing-rules); [METHODS §5](METHODS.md#5-consolidation-verification-and-rating) |
| **Probe** | 探测（`uie probe`） | A scripted interaction run through `uie probe` that returns screenshots, an ARIA snapshot, console and network summaries and timing; black-box roles and the verifier rely on it, because a behavioural claim that was never exercised is only a hypothesis. | [ARCHITECTURE §7.2](ARCHITECTURE.md#72-commands); [FRAMEWORK §8.1](FRAMEWORK.md#81-writing-rules) |
| **Run scope** | 运行范围 | The routes × states × viewport widths (320, 375, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440 by default) × shipped themes × preference emulations that a run covers, with state recipes opening menus, dialogs and error states; coverage below the declared scope makes the affected criteria `degraded`. | [QUALITY-BAR §2.4](QUALITY-BAR.md#24-scope-of-a-run); [ARCHITECTURE §9](ARCHITECTURE.md#9-evidence-pipeline) |
| **Language lint** | 措辞检查（建议译名） | The `uie report` check that report wording stays within the evidence: no "users …" below E3, no causal verbs below E5, no completeness claims such as "WCAG compliant" and no synthetic metrics (EVD-06). | [ARCHITECTURE §10.3](ARCHITECTURE.md#103-language-lint); [FRAMEWORK §13](FRAMEWORK.md#13-honesty-rules-for-every-output) |
| **Simulated** | 模拟（标签） | The label on persona walkthroughs (scripted power users, first-timers, screen-reader users and the like), simulated pilots and LLM task completions, whose outputs generate hypotheses only, never exceed E1 and are never reported as user behaviour, predicted success or metrics. | [FRAMEWORK §4.4](FRAMEWORK.md#44-evidence-levels); [METHODS §4.7](METHODS.md#47-optional-inspection-methods); [ADR-028](DECISIONS.md#adr-028--simulated-users-generate-hypotheses-only) |

## 4. Roles and independence

角色与独立性

| Term | 中文 | Definition | Defined in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lead** | 主代理（建议译名） | The agent instance that talks to the user and orchestrates: it spawns roles, merges results, decides with the owner and also acts as builder and fixer. | [FRAMEWORK §1](FRAMEWORK.md#1-conventions), [§6.1](FRAMEWORK.md#61-roles) |
| **Owner** | 负责人（产品负责人） | The human who owns the product decision, usually the user, who alone grants waivers and who also confirms findings (E2) and runs studies. | [FRAMEWORK §1](FRAMEWORK.md#1-conventions), [§6.1](FRAMEWORK.md#61-roles) |
| **Heuristic evaluator** | 启发式评估员 | A black-box role, three or more per standard audit, that inspects the rendered UI against H1–H10 with its context packet, first in a flow pass and then in an element pass, and returns candidate findings without severity. | [FRAMEWORK §6.1](FRAMEWORK.md#61-roles); [ARCHITECTURE §6.1](ARCHITECTURE.md#61-roster) |
| **Walkthrough evaluator** | 走查评估员 | A black-box role that walks one journey with its persona and correct action sequence fixed in advance, answering the four CW questions at every step with a success or failure story. | [FRAMEWORK §6.1](FRAMEWORK.md#61-roles) |
| **Design critic** | 设计评论员（建议译名） | A black-box panel role that sees screenshots, `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md`, writes its specificity and appeal verdicts before the detector section of its packet is unsealed, and then scores the rubric and records design findings. | [FRAMEWORK §6.1](FRAMEWORK.md#61-roles); [METHODS §4.4](METHODS.md#44-design-panel) |
| **Accessibility auditor** | 无障碍审核员 | A grey-box role that interprets automated results, resolves axe `incomplete` items and judges what tools cannot (colour-only meaning, forced colours, reading and focus order, ARIA semantics), producing the needs-human list. | [FRAMEWORK §6.1](FRAMEWORK.md#61-roles); [METHODS §4.5](METHODS.md#45-accessibility-audit) |
| **Code reviewer** | 代码审查员 | The white-box role that reads source and lint output, reports code-level findings (tokens, states, semantics, IME, i18n) with file:line and estimates ease of fix, without judging the UX. | [FRAMEWORK §6.1](FRAMEWORK.md#61-roles); [METHODS §4.6](METHODS.md#46-code-review-white-box) |
| **Finding verifier** | 发现项核验员（建议译名） | The skeptical role that runs every candidate through the verification chain without knowing who raised it and confirms or rejects it with a reason. | [FRAMEWORK §6.1](FRAMEWORK.md#61-roles); [METHODS §5](METHODS.md#5-consolidation-verification-and-rating) |
| **Severity rater** | 严重程度评分员 | One of at least three blind raters who score the factors and the 0–4 severity of every confirmed judged finding. | [FRAMEWORK §6.1](FRAMEWORK.md#61-roles); [ADR-007](DECISIONS.md#adr-007--severity-per-finding-rated-blind-by-three-or-more-raters) |
| **Fix reviewer** | 修复复核员（建议译名） | A fresh role that rates each claimed fix `confirmed_fixed`, `partially_fixed` or `not_fixed` from baseline and current evidence alone, ignoring the fixer's narration, and names up to three regressions; `partially_fixed` or `not_fixed` can never lead to "ship". | [FRAMEWORK §6.1](FRAMEWORK.md#61-roles), [§10](FRAMEWORK.md#10-fix-discipline) |
| **Direction designer** | 方向设计员（建议译名） | A role that elaborates one dealt direction in isolation, without seeing the others, into a direction contract, a token sketch and an optional specimen page. | [FRAMEWORK §6.1](FRAMEWORK.md#61-roles); [METHODS §6](METHODS.md#6-direction-setting) |
| **Participant** | 参与者 | A real person taking part in a study and the only source of E3–E5 evidence, identified by a code, asked for informed consent and paid a flat incentive that never depends on performance. | [FRAMEWORK §6.1](FRAMEWORK.md#61-roles), [§9.3](FRAMEWORK.md#93-participants) |

## 6. Empirical methods and statistics
