What we can and can’t fix

If you can carry it in, there’s a good chance we can help.

We can fix

  • Small electricals

    Toasters, kettles, radios, hairdryers, food mixers and fans.

  • Lamps and lights

    Table and floor lamps, fairy lights, frayed flexes, loose plugs and dodgy switches.

  • Bikes

    Punctures, brakes, gears, chains, wobbly wheels, lights and bells.

  • Clothes and fabric

    Hems, seams, holes, zips, buttons and patches. Bags, cushions and soft toys too.

We can’t fix

For safety, or because we don’t have the skills or the space:

  • Microwaves – they can hold a dangerous charge even when unplugged.
  • Anything with gas or petrol – heaters, camping stoves, lawnmowers, strimmers.
  • E-bike and e-scooter batteries – lithium batteries are a fire risk.
  • Phones, laptops, tablets and TVs – we don’t have volunteers for these yet.
  • Anything too big to carry in – washing machines, fridges, cookers, furniture.

Not sure? Email a photo to repaircafe@example.org and we’ll tell you if it’s worth bringing.

How it works

  1. Book a slot

    Choose a half-hour arrival time, so we’re not all queuing at 10. No booking? Drop in anyway – people with a slot are seen first.

  2. Bring it along

    Please bring it clean – crumbs out of the toaster, clothes washed – plus any chargers, spare parts or instructions you have.

  3. Fix it together

    Sit with a volunteer while they work and see how it’s done. Repairs are free, and donations help keep us going.

We can’t promise every repair will work, but we’ll always give it a good go. Repairs are done at your own risk, and you stay responsible for your item.
