#### Principles (2026 re-introduction)
On 2026-06-08 Apple reintroduced a principles page with eight principles, each a one-line imperative plus 2–4 sub-rules (design-principles):

| Principle | One-line gist (paraphrased) | Sub-rules that are evaluable |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | make something meaningful | identify what matters most; keep focused on core features; don't recreate existing solutions—find what sets you apart |
| Agency | let people do things their own way | stay out of the way; no locked flows—guided flows must be skippable/escapable; build forgiveness (reversible actions) |
| Responsibility | act in people's best interest | explain why when asking permission; collect only what's needed; anticipate misuse (WWDC26 adds: AI features need safeguards—previews, confirmations, or removal if risk > value) |
| Familiarity | build on what people know | use known metaphors; consistent visuals and interactions (WWDC26: identical-looking elements must behave identically); clear feedback |
| Flexibility | adapt to contexts and needs | accessibility from the start; keep controls in predictable positions across sizes; support voice/touch/keyboard; equal care per platform |
| Simplicity | be clear and direct | "Simplicity isn't minimalism"; be concise; establish hierarchy so the most important item is always the most obvious (WWDC26) |
| Craft | care about every detail | quality in visuals/animation/wording/audio; prototype and iterate; test in real settings; maintain over time |
| Delight | make it human | identify the target emotion; create defining moments; **delight is not decoration** |

The WWDC26 talk is unusually direct about anti-patterns that matter for "AI slop": delight is *not* "adding confetti or tacking on extra flourishes at the end"; a minimal-looking UI that buries functions in one place is not simple; craft failures are named concretely (button tap with no response, jittery scrolling, misaligned icons, layout breaking on rotation). WWDC17 "Essential Design Principles" (Mike Stern) supplies the classic evaluative vocabulary: wayfinding questions (where am I, where can I go, what will I find there, what's nearby, how do I get out), feedback types (status, completion, warning, error) answering four questions — what can I do, what just happened, what is happening now, what will happen next — visibility, external + internal consistency, mental models, proximity, mapping, affordance (perceived, person-relative), progressive disclosure (80/20), symmetry.

#### Accessibility (rev. 2025-06-09)
- Text: support ≥200 % enlargement (140 % watchOS). Default/minimum text sizes: iOS/iPadOS 17/11 pt; macOS 13/10 pt; tvOS 29/23; visionOS 17/12; watchOS 16/12. Thin weights need larger sizes.
- Contrast: cites both WCAG and APCA. Accessibility Inspector's WCAG-AA table: ≤17 pt → 4.5:1; 18 pt → 3:1; bold (any size) → 3:1. **Note:** treating bold text of any size as 3:1 is looser than WCAG's large-text definition (≥18 pt regular or ≥14 pt bold) — see conflicts. If defaults miss the minimum, an Increase-Contrast scheme must meet it; check light *and* dark.
- Dark Mode page adds: never below 4.5:1, and **strive for 7:1 for custom foreground/background colours, especially small text**.
- Targets: default/minimum control size iOS/iPadOS 44×44 / 28×28 pt; macOS 28×28 / 20×20; tvOS 66×66 / 56×56; visionOS 60×60 / 28×28; watchOS 44×44 / 28×28. Spacing: ~12 pt padding around bezelled elements, ~24 pt around bezel-less elements.
- Never colour alone (red-green and blue-orange named); gesture alternatives (e.g., swipe-to-delete must also have a button); keyboard-only operation without overriding system shortcuts; minimise auto-dismissing (time-boxed) UI; no autoplay without controls; Reduce Motion response: tighten springs, track gestures directly, avoid z-axis depth animation, replace x/y/z transitions with fades, avoid animating into/out of blur.
- Assistive Access: one interaction per screen; confirm hard-to-reverse actions twice.

#### Colour, Dark Mode, materials, Liquid Glass
- Don't use one colour for two meanings (e.g., brand colour for interactive *and* non-interactive text). Supply light, dark, and increased-contrast variants for every custom colour; test under different lighting and on P3 vs sRGB. Colour meaning is cultural (red = danger vs. luck).
- Semantic/dynamic colours are defined by *purpose* (label, secondaryLabel, tertiaryLabel, quaternaryLabel, placeholderText, separator, opaqueSeparator, link; system/grouped backgrounds in primary/secondary/tertiary levels). Don't repurpose semantics (no separator colour as text). Don't hard-code system colour values.
- Dark Mode is not an inversion; base vs elevated backgrounds (elevated = brighter, for layers in front); soften white-background images; avoid an app-specific appearance toggle (respect the system setting) — a point of tension with common web practice.
- Liquid Glass (2025): a functional layer for controls/navigation floating above content. Rules: not in the content layer; use sparingly; no glass-on-glass; regular variant for text-heavy components; clear variant only over media, with a **35 % dark dimming layer** if content is bright. Tint only primary actions ("when every element is tinted, nothing stands out" — WWDC25 #219, the one quote used from that session); put brand colour in the content layer. Increased Contrast makes glass mostly black/white with a border; Reduce Motion removes elastic properties.
- Brand colour (rev. 2026-09-09): apply the accent judiciously—primary actions and status indicators (unread badge, selected tab), not on every control. Logo repetition and launch-screen branding are discouraged; branding must defer to content; express brand through familiar components.
- **Concentric geometry (WWDC25 #356):** three shape types — fixed radius; capsule (radius = ½ height); concentric (child radius = parent radius − padding). Watch for corners that look "pinched" or "flared" in nested containers. Hierarchy should come from layout and grouping rather than added decoration (backgrounds/borders on bar items); don't group a text button and an icon button in one shared background; scroll-edge effects are functional, not decorative; at most one scroll-edge effect per view.

#### Typography
- iOS default ("Large") Dynamic Type styles (size/leading pt): Large Title 34/41, Title1 28/34, Title2 22/28, Title3 20/25, Headline 17/22 semibold, Body 17/22, Callout 16/21, Subhead 15/20, Footnote 13/18, Caption1 12/16, Caption2 11/13. Ratios ≈ 1.18–1.3 line-height; size steps are small (≈1.06–1.2×). AX5 Body reaches 53/62 pt. macOS: Body 13/16, Large Title 26/32.
- Avoid Ultralight/Thin/Light weights for UI text; minimise typefaces; prioritise what grows when text size grows; keep hierarchy at every size; avoid truncation at large sizes; stack inline items vertically and reduce columns at accessibility sizes; tight leading never for ≥3 lines.
- SF Pro tracking table: positive at small sizes (+41/1000 em at 6 pt), zero at 12 pt, most negative around 17–20 pt (−26 to −23/1000 em), and **positive again ≥24 pt** (+14/1000 em at 28–30 pt). Lesson: tracking is font- and optical-size-specific; the web habit of applying tight negative tracking to every large heading is not universal.
- Custom fonts: fine for headings, keep system font for body/captions; must support Dynamic Type and Bold Text.

#### Layout, icons, RTL
- Order by importance (top, leading side); align to aid scanning; indentation = subordination; group via space, containers or separators; progressive disclosure; differentiate controls from content.
- Lay out by size class (available space), not device or orientation; keep functionality constant across sizes (only *visibility* of functions may change, e.g., tab bar → sidebar). Respect safe areas. tvOS safe area 60 pt top/bottom, 80 pt sides. visionOS button centres ≥60 pt apart. macOS: no critical controls at the window bottom.
- Interface icons: consistent size, detail level, stroke weight and perspective across the set; match icon weight to adjacent text; optical (not geometric) centering via padding; text inside icons only when essential and localised; vector format; alt text; standard symbols for standard actions (e.g., trash = delete only). Menus: icons for all items in a group or none.
- RTL: mirror navigation/progress controls; never reverse digits inside a number; don't flip photos, logos, checkmarks, clocks; paragraph (≥3 lines) aligns by its own language; Arabic/Hebrew next to all-caps Latin may need ~+2 pt.

#### Writing and content
- Pick a voice; vary tone by situation; build a term list; sentence vs title case chosen per element type and applied consistently; button/link labels start with a plain verb rather than a cute phrase; avoid "click here"; avoid "we" in errors (state the problem impersonally); use possessives sparingly; correct device verbs (tap vs click); empty states give a next step + button; error messages near the problem, no blame, say how to fix (e.g., state the 8-character minimum), no "oops"; hint text in fields; describe settings by what "on" does; link directly to settings.
- Inclusion: address people as "you", avoid "the user"; define jargon; avoid colloquialisms and gratuitous humour (hard to translate); gender-neutral copy and figures; inclusive form options (nonbinary / self-identify / decline).
- **Internal inconsistency found:** the Buttons page asks for title-style capitalization in button labels, while the Alerts page tells authors to use sentence-style capitalization for all button titles. (Both read 2026-10-01.)

#### Patterns and components (evaluable rules)
- Feedback: match interruption level to significance; status inline near the item; alerts only for critical, ideally actionable info; warn only for *unexpected* irreversible loss (Finder doesn't warn on trash); confirm only significant successes; say why a command can't run.
- Loading: show something immediately (placeholders); let people do other things meanwhile; determinate indicator when duration is known; indeterminate otherwise.
- Onboarding: fast, fun, optional; teach by doing; prefer contextual tips; don't re-show a skipped tutorial but keep it findable; defer setup with good defaults; ask permissions in context with a reason; don't ask for ratings/purchases before engagement.
- Modality: only with clear benefit; short and simple; no nested mini-app hierarchy inside a modal; always an obvious dismiss; confirm before discarding user content; title names the task; one modal at a time, never two alerts.
- Searching: one primary search location; show scope; suggestions/recents; allow clearing history.
- Settings: minimise settings; good defaults; don't duplicate system-wide settings (appearance, accessibility); task options belong in context.
- Entering data: don't ask what you can infer; label + example hint; never prefill passwords; prefer choices over typing; allow paste/drag; **validate dynamically**; make Next available only when required data is present (conflicts with GOV.UK—see §3).
- Buttons: hit region ≥44×44 pt (visionOS 60×60); every custom button needs a press state; 1–2 prominent buttons per view; distinguish the preferred option by *style, not size*; never give the primary role to a destructive action; in-button spinner + changed label ("Checking out…") for delayed actions; ellipsis on labels that open another view.
- Menus: verb labels, no articles, ellipsis when more input is needed, dimmed (not hidden) unavailable items, most-used first, related items grouped with separators, submenus one level deep and ≲5 items.
- Lists/tables: succinct row text; middle-ellipsis to keep start and end; column headings are nouns, title case, no punctuation; persistent highlight for navigational selection, checkmark for option selection.
- Sheets: one sheet at a time; Done always paired with Cancel (or Back); never all three.
- Alerts: sparingly; never informational-only; not on app launch; not for common undoable actions; title ≤2 lines and specific (never a bare "Error" or an error code); 1–2-word verb button titles; "OK" only for purely informational alerts; "Cancel" always named Cancel, never the default; default/likely button trailing (or top of stack); destructive style only for destructive actions the person didn't explicitly choose; Esc / Cmd-. cancels.

**Strengths.** Very concrete numbers (targets, type sizes, tracking, safe areas); principled and recently refreshed (2025–2026); strong on writing, modality and feedback; WWDC26 talk explicitly rejects decoration-as-delight.
**Weaknesses/gaps.** Native-first: sizes are in points, not CSS px; no motion durations/easings (only qualitative guidance); system colour values exist only as images; some internal inconsistencies (button capitalization); Liquid Glass guidance is SwiftUI-centric and its translucency is risky for web contrast; no spacing scale.
**Adopt.** Principles as review lenses (esp. simplicity ≠ minimalism and delight ≠ decoration); target sizes and spacing; contrast in both themes + 7:1 aspiration for small custom text; concentric radius rule; one-prominent-action rule; alert/modal/feedback rules; writing rules; reduce-motion substitutions.

#### Layout (I/O 2026 "Expressive layout" refresh)
- Breakpoints (formerly window size classes; Android *and web*): **Compact <600dp, Medium 600–839, Expanded 840–1199, Large 1200–1599, Extra-large ≥1600** (breakpoints, updated 2026-07-17).
- Panes: compact 1; medium 1 (2 only for low-density content); expanded/large 2 recommended; XL up to 3. Fixed pane width 360dp (expanded) / 412dp (large+). Margins: 16dp compact; 24dp medium and up; 24dp spacer between panes.
- Five adaptation questions when crossing a breakpoint: what to **reveal, divide, resize, reposition, swap**; swaps must be functionally equivalent (never button↔chip). Keep text at **40–60 characters per line** across breakpoints.
- Canonical layouts: feed, list-detail, supporting pane (primary ≈ 2/3 width).
- Grids & spacing: explicit grouping (outlines, dividers, shadows) vs implicit grouping (proximity, whitespace); rhythm, similarity (leading thumbnails/avatars/icons always aligned), proximity (buttons near what they affect), continuity; *spacing as expression*: generous space + brightest surface for the most important content; rulers (bar/safety, title, content) for global alignment.
- **Spacing system (new, 2026):** 8dp linear scale; `md.sys.measurement.space100 = 8dp`, multiplier naming (space125 = 10dp, space225 = 18dp); nested units 2/4/6/10dp allowed; padding (inside) / gap (between) / margin (outside) with logical directions (leading/trailing). Rule: prefer padding and gaps over margins inside components. **Spacing should be preserved when text scales to 200 %.** Tokens are Compose-only for now.
- Density: scale 0, −1, −2, −3 (each step ≈ −4dp height); density must be user-controlled, not automatically changed by breakpoint; **don't apply density that pushes targets below 48×48 CSS px**.

#### Interaction states
- Six states: enabled, disabled, hover, focused, pressed, dragged (+ activated/selected). Each state needs **two visual indicators** for accessibility. A **state layer** is an overlay in the *content* colour at fixed opacity: **hover 8 %, focus 10 %, pressed 10 %, dragged 16 %** (current Compose `StateTokens`; the frozen material-web tokens `tokens/versions/v0_192/_md-sys-state.scss` still use **12 %** for focus and pressed). State layer 40dp inside a 48dp target. Activated/selected use container colour change (secondary container), not a state layer.
- Disabled: not focusable/hoverable/pressable; **disabled states are exempt from contrast requirements**; disabled opacities are **38 % for label/icon and 12 % for container** (material-web `v0_192/_md-comp-filled-button.scss`, verified).
- Keyboard: visible ring-style focus indicator — material-web focus ring = **3px width, 2px outward offset, `secondary` colour, fully rounded**, briefly animating to 8px (`_md-comp-focus-ring.scss`); only one hover and one focus at a time; set initial focus; on dialog open move focus into it, on close return focus to the trigger; single-key shortcuts must be remappable or scoped (designing page).

#### Accessibility numbers
- Touch targets **≥48×48dp (~9 mm)**; pointer targets ≥44×44dp; targets separated by **≥8dp**. Icons are typically 24dp inside a 48dp target. XS/S icon buttons still need 48dp targets.
- Contrast: 4.5:1 small text, 3:1 large text (≥14 pt bold / 18 pt) and graphics. Clustered controls (e.g., a group of buttons) need 3:1 container-vs-background; standalone prominent elements (FAB) are exempt; disabled exempt.
- MCU encodes contrast per role at the default contrast level: on-surface ≥ **7:1** (discretionary), on-primary ≥7:1, on-*-container ≥4.5:1, primary vs surface ≥4.5:1, outline ≥3:1, outline-variant decorative only. MCU's guaranteed default minimum is 4.5:1 for all text/icons and 3:1 for required non-text. Medium/high contrast levels raise these (on-surface up to 11:1 / 21:1). Heuristic: **a tone (L\*) difference of 40 guarantees ≥3:1; 50 guarantees ≥4.5:1** (MCU concepts/contrast_for_accessibility.md).

#### Colour system
- 26 standard roles in six groups (primary, secondary, tertiary, error, surface, outline) + add-ons (fixed, fixed-dim, surface-dim/bright, inverse). Naming grammar: `X` / `on-X` / `X-container` / `on-X-container` / `*-variant`. Containers are fills, never text. Only `on-` pairs are guaranteed accessible.
- Tonal palettes on HCT (hue, chroma, tone); tones 0–100 (steps of 10 plus 95/98/99). Baseline mapping (light/dark): surface 98/6, on-surface 10/90, primary 40/80, on-primary 100/20, primary-container 90/30, surface-container lowest→highest light 100/96/94/92/90, dark 4/10/12/17/22; outline 50/60; outline-variant 80/30 (MCU color_spec_2021).
- Surfaces are tone-based, not elevation-tinted (since Feb 2023). Error is static (doesn't follow dynamic colour). Three user-selectable contrast levels (standard/medium/high). Keep surface mappings constant for a layout region across breakpoints.
- Use roles, never raw palette values, in components; HCT is not HSL (don't mix colour spaces when checking).

#### Typography
- 15 baseline + 15 emphasized styles. Baseline (size/line-height sp, weight, tracking): Display L 57/64 (−0.2), M 45/52, S 36/44; Headline L 32/40, M 28/36, S 24/32; Title L 22/28, M 16/24 medium (+0.15–0.2), S 14/20 medium (+0.1); Body L 16/24 (+0.5), M 14/20 (+0.2), S 12/16 (+0.4); Label L 14/20 medium (+0.1), M 12/16 medium (+0.5), S 11/16 medium (+0.5) (Compose TypeScaleTokens). Emphasized = one weight step heavier.
- Scale stated as **Major Second (1.125) with base 14**; "avoid small differences" between adjacent sizes. Brand typeface for display/headline, plain for body/label. Fallback chain for variable fonts (Roboto Flex → Roboto → Noto). sp→rem = /16.
- **Language-height support (Aug 2026):** line height adapts by script category — small (Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Hebrew), **medium ≈7 % taller (Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Thai, Hindi, Vietnamese…)**, large ≈30 % (Burmese, Telugu), XL ≈100 % (Nastaliq). Default to medium; fixed-height components break for taller scripts.

#### Shape and elevation
- Corner scale (10 steps): none 0, XS 4, S 8, M 12, L 16, L-increased 20, XL 28, XL-increased 32, XXL 48, full. **Optical roundness: inner radius = outer radius − padding** (e.g., 48 − 14 = 34). Abstract/expressive shapes (35-shape library) only for mostly-visual elements, never on text-heavy containers; shape is not semantic.
- Elevation levels 0–5 = 0, 1, 3, 6, 8, 12dp. Default depiction is **tonal surface difference**, shadows only to protect elements on busy backgrounds or to signal interaction; hover raises one level; few levels (fewer levels keep each one meaningful); scrim = scrim role at **32 %** opacity.

#### Motion
- **Physics system (M3 Expressive, May 2025) replaces easing/duration** for components; springs = stiffness + damping. Two schemes: *expressive* (default, slight overshoot) and *standard* (utilitarian, minimal bounce). Tokens: spatial (position/size/rotation/corners; may overshoot) vs effects (colour/opacity; no overshoot) × fast/default/slow. Expressive spatial damping 0.8 (fast 0.6) with stiffness 380 (fast 800, slow 200); standard spatial damping 0.9, stiffness 700/1400/300; effects damping 1.0, stiffness 1600/3800/800.
- **Official web conversions** (spring → CSS cubic-bezier + duration): expressive fast spatial `cubic-bezier(0.42,1.67,0.21,0.90)` 350 ms; expressive default spatial `(0.38,1.21,0.22,1.00)` 500 ms; slow spatial `(0.39,1.29,0.35,0.98)` 650 ms; effects fast `(0.31,0.94,0.34,1.00)` 150 ms, default `(0.34,0.80,0.34,1.00)` 200 ms, slow `(0.34,0.88,0.34,1.00)` 300 ms; standard spatial `(0.27,1.06,0.18,1.00)` 350/500/750 ms.
- Transition patterns (still on the legacy system): container transform, forward/backward, lateral, **top level = fade-through** (unrelated destinations get no spatial link), enter/exit (components expand away from the edge they live at — top menus grow down, bottom snackbars grow up), skeleton loaders.
- Legacy easing/duration (still used for transitions): emphasized 500 ms (begin+end on screen), emphasized-decelerate `(0.05,0.7,0.1,1)` 400 ms (enter), emphasized-accelerate `(0.3,0,0.8,0.15)` 200 ms (exit); standard `(0.2,0,0,1)` 300 ms, standard-decelerate `(0,0,0,1)` 250 ms, standard-accelerate `(0.3,0,1,1)` 200 ms. Durations short 50–200, medium 250–400, long 450–600, extra-long 700–1000 ms. Rules: duration scales with area travelled; **exits shorter than entrances**; permanent exits accelerate out, temporary exits come to rest.

#### Content design (style-guide, 2026-08-04)
- Global writing: translations run ~1.5× longer than English; short sentences; no abbreviations; repeat nouns instead of ambiguous pronouns; avoid words that are both noun and verb in one string; avoid please/sorry/thank-you in errors (please only when asking for something inconvenient).
- Sentence case for **all** UI text (titles, buttons, menus, navigation); "you" not "we"; don't mix "my" and "your"; skip periods in single-sentence labels, tooltips, bullets, dialog body; contractions OK; serial comma; **no ellipses on buttons/menu items that open a dialog** (direct conflict with Apple); avoid em dashes; avoid all-caps blocks; avoid Latin abbreviations (e.g., etc.); exclamation marks sparingly; explain consequences neutrally.

#### M3 Expressive and its research claims
- Claims (Google-reported): 46 studies, 18,000+ participants over ~3 years; expressive designs preferred across ages (up to 87 % among 18–24); people found key elements "up to four times faster"; age gap in visual search erased for 45+; perception gains in "modernity" (+34 %) etc. Methods named: eye-tracking, surveys, focus groups, usability tests.
- Caveats (also partly stated by Google): context-dependent (not for e.g. banking); unfamiliar patterns hurt usability; "up to" figures are best cases; no stimuli, effect sizes, confidence intervals or peer review are published → treat as **vendor evidence, not generalisable proof** (and an instance of the lecture's caution that correlation is not causation and analyses must be reported).
- Tactics: varied shapes, rich colour roles, emphasized type, containment for emphasis, fluid motion, component flexibility, and **"hero moments" — limit to one or two per product**. Usability page: the main CTA should be the largest element; one primary task per page; don't stack too many expressive tactics.

**Strengths.** Best token-level rigour of any system (colour algorithm with explicit contrast curves; spring→CSS conversion table; state-layer opacities); explicit breakpoints and pane rules; script-aware line heights; content style guide.
**Weaknesses.** Web implementation frozen; many values live only in Compose tokens; the guideline site is hard to cite by URL (SPA); vendor research claims lack published data; expressive shapes invite decorative misuse ("blob" aesthetics are a recognisable template look).
**Adopt.** Breakpoints; 48px target / 8px spacing; state-layer model; role-based colour with `on-` pairs; tone-delta contrast heuristic; optical-roundness rule; tonal elevation first, shadows rarely; spring→bezier table; exit<enter durations; language-height adjustment for CJK; sentence case; hero-moment budget.

### 2.4 IBM Carbon (v11)

**What it is.** IBM's open-source product design system (React/Web Components) built on the IBM Design Language and IBM Plex. **URLs read (https://carbondesignsystem.com/...):** `elements/2x-grid/overview` (updated 2026-09-25), `elements/2x-grid/usage`, `elements/typography/{overview,type-sets,style-strategies}`, `elements/motion/{overview,choreography}`, `elements/color/{overview,usage,tokens}`, `elements/spacing/overview`, `elements/themes/overview`, `guidelines/accessibility/overview`, `guidelines/content/overview`, `patterns/{disabled-states,loading-pattern,empty-states-pattern,notification-pattern,forms-pattern,status-indicator-pattern}`, `all-about-carbon/what-is-carbon`.
**License.** carbon repo **Apache-2.0**; IBM Plex fonts **SIL OFL 1.1** (google/fonts `ofl/ibmplexsans`). Site text © IBM → paraphrase.

**2x Grid.** 8px "mini unit"; divide-by-two (fluid) or multiply-by-two (fixed) logic. Breakpoints: **sm 320 (4 cols, margin 0), md 672 (8 cols, 16), lg 1056 (16 cols, 16), xlg 1312 (16, 16), max 1584 (16, 24)**; box padding 16px at all breakpoints, so total gutter 32px (gutterless allowed for closely related content). Aspect ratios limited to 1:1, 2:1, 2:3, 3:2, 4:3, 16:9. Fixed sizing scale 8/16/24/32/48/64/80. Rules worth checking: **type sits against the box padding, never inside it**; layouts must show visible key lines (shared vertical and horizontal alignment lines); choose fluid columns when users want bigger things, fixed tiles when they want more things; hybrid boxes for header/toolbar (fluid width, fixed height) and side panels (fixed width).

**Spacing.** `$spacing-01…13` = 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96, 160px; tokens are static but you may step down a level at smaller breakpoints; prefer a Stack/gap component over margins; individual sections may be dense, but never the whole page.

**Typography.** IBM Plex Sans/Serif/Mono. Scale from one formula, y₀ = 12: Xₙ = Xₙ₋₁ + (⌊(n−2)/4⌋ + 1)·2 → 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 42, 48, 54, 60, 68, 76, 84, 92 (increments grow by 2 every four steps). **Two type sets:** *productive* (base 14px; body-compact-01 14/18, body-01 14/20, heading-01 14/20 semibold, heading-03 20/28, heading-04 28/36, heading-05 32/40, heading-06 42/50 light, heading-07 54/64 light; fixed sizes, for dense task UI) and *expressive* (base 16px; body-02 16/24, fluid headings that scale between breakpoints, display 54/64+; for editorial/marketing). Weights Light 300 / Regular 400 / SemiBold 600 only; semibold not for long text; Light only at large sizes. Body copy ≥4 lines uses the taller "long" line height and is always left-aligned. Blending rule: an expressive "moment" in a product must span a full page/banner, not sit inside containers; productive styles inside web pages for focused tasks (forms, filters, commerce).

**Colour.** Grey-dominant themes (White, Gray 10, Gray 90, Gray 100 named after their background: #ffffff, #f4f4f4, #262626, #161616); Blue 60 = primary action; other hues sparing. **Layering model:** light themes alternate White ↔ Gray 10 per layer; dark themes go one step lighter per layer; never place components darker than the background (except high-contrast mode); avoid mid-tones. Token grammar `<element>-<role>[-state]` (`$text-secondary`, `$layer-hover`). **State maths on a 10-grade palette:** hover = a half step (lighter for 70–100, darker for 10–60), selected = one full step, active = two full steps, focus = **2px border, Blue 60 (light) / White (dark), must reach 3:1** (use `$focus-inset` to guarantee it). **Contrast by grade distance:** e.g., Gray 100 needs Gray 50 or lighter (5 steps) for 4.5:1 and Gray 60 (4 steps) for 3:1 — a "magic-number" check that needs no calculator. Carbon simplifies large text to ">24px" (stricter than WCAG's 18.66px-bold clause).

**Motion.** Two styles: **productive** (efficient, subtle; default for micro-interactions) and **expressive** (vibrant; reserved for significant moments — page open, primary action, system alerts). Curves: standard productive `cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0.38,0.9)` / expressive `(0.4,0.14,0.3,1)`; entrance productive `(0,0,0.38,0.9)` / expressive `(0,0,0.3,1)`; exit productive `(0.2,0,1,0.9)` / expressive `(0.4,0.14,1,1)`. Durations: fast-01 70ms (buttons, toggles), fast-02 110ms (fades), moderate-01 150ms, moderate-02 240ms (expansion, toasts), slow-01 400ms (large expansion), slow-02 700ms (background dimming); duration grows non-linearly with distance/size. **Evaluation checklist** (directly reusable): purposeful (what problem does it solve?), responsive (**micro-interactions 90–120ms, ease-out on input**), meticulous (right curve, related motions unified, works on mobile), **unobtrusive (motion that average users notice often should be reduced or removed)**. Choreography: motion paths along the grid, never diagonal; same meaning → same motion; forward = affirm, reverse = cancel; **stagger ≈20ms per item with total ≤500ms**; entrance order: shell → static body → dynamic data → primary action → data-viz animation.

**Patterns (evaluable).**
- Disabled: three variants — default disabled (visible, not focusable, component 50 % / text 25 % / icon 50 % opacity, no hover, `not-allowed` cursor), read-only (readable by screen readers, no interactive styling), hidden (permission-based). If a disabled control blocks the main flow, add an inline warning explaining how to enable it.
- Loading: skeletons only for container/data components (tiles, tables, cards), never for toasts, menus, dropdown items, modals; skeletons should last only seconds; indeterminate loader only for brief waits, progress indicator for longer; full-screen loader only when the whole app is blocked; announce loading/busy/failure to screen readers.
- Empty states: optional image + positive title ("Start by adding data assets") + body explaining next action + primary action (or point at the UI element) + optional secondary link; types: no-data/first-use, user-action (no results, completion), error-management (permissions, system, configuration); left-aligned block; with several empty states on one screen use tertiary buttons (avoid many primaries); the empty state *replaces* the empty component (no orphan table headers).
- Notifications: inline (near the item, ≤2 lines, persistent), toast (≤3 lines, fixed width, newest on top; auto-dismiss only if it has no action), actionable (one action, ≤2-word label, persists, takes focus), callout (static guidance, not dismissible), banner (one at a time, scrolls with page), modal (critical only, one at a time). Limit interruptions to avoid alert fatigue.
- Forms: top-aligned labels of 1–3 words, no colons; mark the minority (only "(optional)" when most are required, or only "(required)" when most are optional) and be consistent product-wide; helper text for need-to-know info (always visible), placeholder only for format hints (never instead of a label); >5 options → select/combobox; good defaults; first required field gets focus; button alignment by context (in-page left-aligned with primary first; wizards/dialogs right-aligned with primary last; dialogs/side panels full-bleed).

**Strengths.** The most explicit **productive vs expressive** split (type and motion) — a ready-made answer to when expressiveness is appropriate; grid and spacing are fully numeric; colour-state arithmetic; concrete pattern rules and an evaluation checklist for motion.
**Weaknesses.** Strongly IBM-branded grey/blue aesthetic (copying it wholesale produces an "enterprise template" look); large-text definition simplified; some guidance still marked as needing research (banners, notification panel).
**Adopt.** Productive/expressive mode switch; motion checklist and 90–120ms micro-interaction band; stagger ≤500ms total; layering model; grade-distance contrast heuristic; disabled taxonomy; skeleton rules; empty-state anatomy; notification persistence rules; form-label/optional-marking rules; key-line alignment check.


### 2.5 Shopify Polaris (now web components) + Shopify App Design Guidelines

**What it is.** Shopify's admin design system. **Status change (verified 2026-10-01):** polaris.shopify.com now redirects to https://shopify.dev/docs/api/polaris; the React library repo (`Shopify/polaris`) is **archived and labelled "Deprecated"**; Polaris is now Shopify's unified, web-components-based UI framework across App Home, admin, checkout, customer-account and POS extensions. Design guidance now lives in the App Design Guidelines. **URLs read:** https://shopify.dev/docs/api/polaris, https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design, `/visual-design`, `/content`, `/content/voice-and-tone`, `/content/grammar-and-mechanics`, `/layout`, `/navigation`.
**License.** ⚠️ The Polaris code licence is **not plain MIT**: rights may be exercised only for apps that integrate with Shopify, or for stand-alone apps that are "dissimilar and visually distinct" from Shopify products (LICENSE.md in Shopify/polaris). **Do not copy Polaris code/tokens into a general-purpose skill**; cite and paraphrase guidance only.

**Visual rules (numbers).** Minimum text **13px for headings, body and interactive text; 12px for captions/subheadings** (admin context); page title should be the largest heading; headings distinguished by weight/size, never by underline (looks like a link) or colour alone; 4px spacing grid; app icon 1200×1200, content within 10/16–12/16 of the canvas, 1/16 clear margin, no rounded corners (platform rounds it).

**Semantic colour discipline.** Majority of text neutral (black/dark grey). Never colour-only for status. Contrast ≥4.5:1.

Icons used inconsistently in repeated items make an app look broken.

**Voice and tone (very relevant to AI-generated copy).** Voice constant, tone situational; business-casual, plain-spoken; target **US grade-7 reading level**; one term per concept (eliminate synonyms); CTAs start with a strong verb; guide, don't prescribe. Concrete don'ts: generalising success words ("every", "all", "most"); calling routine actions successes (e.g., "successfully added"); assuming celebration (an unearned "Congrats!"); overpromising business outcomes; trailing ellipses as placeholder filler; dramatic error words ("invalid", "fatal", "forbidden", "bad request"); raw error codes. Simple errors must state the fix; serious errors explain impact, apologise within reason, give next steps; routine completion = quiet confirmation (toast "Product saved"). Announcements explain merchant benefit specifically and let people postpone the change.
**Mechanics.** Sentence case for headings, buttons, card titles; contractions; bold only for strong emphasis (never for headings, proper nouns or user input); headings informative, single sentence, no terminal punctuation; conversational surfaces (empty states, home cards) may use articles; avoid links, never "click here", at most one "Learn more" per screen; address form order fixed.

**Strengths.** Best-in-class, example-driven content guidance; crisp semantic-colour rules; card/table/primary-action constraints that are easy to check.
**Weaknesses.** Platform-specific (Shopify admin) and licence-restricted; public numeric token docs are thinner after the React deprecation.
**Adopt (as paraphrased rules).** Status-colour semantics; ≤1 primary per card / none in tables; no text directly on background in app UIs; constant density per page; anti-hype copy rules (no "successfully", no unearned "Congrats", no synonyms, grade-7 target, no scary error words, ≤1 "Learn more").


### 2.6 GOV.UK Design System, Service Manual and Government Design Principles

**What it is.** The UK government's public, research-driven design system (GOV.UK Frontend v6) plus the Service Manual and design principles — the most evidence-oriented of all sources reviewed. **URLs read:** https://www.gov.uk/guidance/government-design-principles (updated 2025-04-02); https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard; https://design-system.service.gov.uk/ `styles/{type-scale,headings,paragraphs,typeface,links,spacing,layout,colour}`, `get-started/focus-states`, `components/{button,error-message,error-summary,text-input,details}`, `patterns/{validation,question-pages,confirmation-pages}`, `accessibility`; Service Manual `design/writing-for-user-interfaces`, `design/form-structure`, `helping-people-to-use-your-service/making-your-service-accessible-an-introduction` (updated 2025-12-17), `user-research/using-moderated-usability-testing`; https://www.gov.uk/guidance/style-guide/a-to-z. Source settings: govuk-frontend `settings/_measurements.scss`, `_media-queries.scss`.
**License.** GOV.UK text: **Open Government Licence v3.0** (copy/adapt with attribution — the most permissive text licence in this survey); govuk-frontend code **MIT**. **GDS Transport / New Transport fonts are restricted to service.gov.uk** — never use them elsewhere.

**Principles (11).** Start with user needs; Do less; Design with data; Do the hard work to make it simple; Iterate, then iterate again; This is for everyone (accessibility outranks elegance); Understand context; Build digital services, not websites; Be consistent, not uniform; Make things open; **Minimise environmental impact** (added April 2025). Principle 4 explicitly separates *looking* simple from *being* simple to use — the same distinction Apple makes between simplicity and minimalism.

**Numbers.**
- Type scale (v6; px): large screens ≥641px — 80/80 (exceptional), 48/50 (heading-xl), 36/40 (heading-l), 27/30, 24/30 (heading-m, body-l), **19/25 (heading-s, body)**, 16/20 (body-s); small screens — 53/55, 32/35, 27/30, 21/25, 21/25, **19/25**, 16/20. v6 *raised* small-screen sizes for legibility; **body text is 19px everywhere**; every line height is a multiple of 5px; output in rem/em for zoom. Lead paragraph (24px) at most once per page; body-s sparingly.
- Spacing scale (responsive, small/large): 0, 5, 10, 15, 15/20, 15/25, 20/30, 25/40, 30/50, 40/60 px; static scale uses the large values. Breakpoints: mobile 320, tablet 641, desktop 769. Width: the layout page states a default maximum page width of 1020px while the source setting `$govuk-page-width` is 960px (the difference matches two 30px gutters — interpretation, not stated); gutter 30px; **two-thirds column so lines stay ≤75 characters**; mobile-first single column.
- Borders: 5px standard, 2px form elements, **focus width 3px**. Focus style: **yellow #ffdd00 background/outline + black (#0b0c0c) thick bottom border** — designed to pass WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.11 on any background (yellow contrasts with dark, black with light).
- Functional colours (current palette): text #0b0c0c, secondary text #484949, link #1a65a6 (hover #0f385c, visited #54319f), border #cecece, input border #0b0c0c, focus #ffdd00, error #ca3535, success #0f7a52, brand #1d70b8, template background #f4f8fb. Use functional colours only for their purpose; never give the palette colours new meanings; tints at 25/50/80/95 %, shades at 25/50 %.

**Interaction and form rules (testable).**
- One default (primary) button per page; secondary buttons sparingly (simplify or split the page instead); primary aligned to the left edge of the form; button text in sentence case describing the action ("Save and continue", "Accept and send"); **"Continue", not "Next"**.
- **Avoid disabled buttons** (they have poor contrast and confuse some users) — only if research shows they help. Prevent accidental double submission (`data-prevent-double-click`) and give progress feedback on slow connections.
- **One thing per page** (one question/decision/piece of information) as the starting point; merge only when research supports it. Question pages need a back link, a page heading (label/legend as h1), and a Continue button; never break the browser back button.
- Mark optional fields "(optional)"; **never mark mandatory fields with asterisks**; allow an "I don't know" answer where valid; ask for information once per journey; question protocol (know why, what for, who, how verified) before adding any question.
- Hint text: one short sentence, no full stop, no links. Errors: **validate on submit, not on blur or while typing** (unless research proves otherwise); keep the user's input; prefix `<title>` with "Error: "; error summary at top with focus moved to it; message next to the field with red bar; use the label's own words; be specific (never generic messages such as *An error occurred*, *This field is required* or *Invalid*); avoid "please", "sorry", "valid/invalid", "forbidden", "illegal", "you forgot", "oops"; turn off HTML5 validation (`novalidate`, no `required`) and always validate server-side.
- Links blue and underlined; avoid opening new tabs (if necessary say "opens in new tab"); no external-link icon; inverse links ≥4.5:1.

**Writing.** Start with fewer words; if the copy has to explain how the interface works, fix the interface instead; one idea per sentence; front-load key words; drop "now", "please" and "thank you" (a plain completion statement is enough); "sorry" only for serious failures; no humour in errors; "**Aim to be boring**" — if people don't notice the copy, it's working; never rely on shape, size, colour or location (don't tell people to press the green button or use the menu on the left); single h1; `<title>` = "h1 – service – GOV.UK"; sentence case everywhere; avoid "eg/ie/etc", italics, "&"; avoid negative contractions (can't, don't) and 've contractions; use "they". **Words to avoid** (plain-English list): agenda, advance, collaborate, combat, commit/pledge, counter, deliver, deploy, dialogue, disincentivise, empower, facilitate, focus, foster, impact (verb), incentivise, initiate, key, land, leverage, liaise, overarching, progress, promote, robust, slim down, streamline, strengthening, tackle, transform, utilise, drive/drive out, going/moving forward, in order to, hub, ring fencing — strikingly close to the vocabulary that marks AI-generated marketing copy.

**Empirical method (Service Manual).** Moderated usability tests of 30–60 min, ≤6 sessions/day, ≥15 min between; tasks with a clear goal, believable, challenging enough, not giving away the answer; think-aloud; moderator mostly silent; reassure participants that the service, not they, is being tested; prefer participants' real data where safe; every service must meet **WCAG 2.2 AA** as a minimum and combine manual + automated + assistive-technology testing.

**Strengths.** Evidence-based defaults with explicit research-gated escape hatches; strongest form/validation/error guidance anywhere; accessible focus style that works on any background; openly licensed text.
**Weaknesses.** Deliberately plain, institutional aesthetic (not a model for brand expression); 19px body and 5px rhythm are tuned for GDS Transport; some rules (no disabled buttons, submit-time validation, no asterisks) conflict with other systems.
**Adopt.** Error-message rules and banned words; submit-time validation default; avoid disabled buttons; one primary per page; one-thing-per-page as a form default; optional-marking rule; dual-colour focus indicator; 75-character line cap; words-to-avoid list; boring-on-purpose functional copy; usability-test protocol.


### 2.7 Atlassian Design System (ADS)

**What it is.** Design system for Jira, Confluence, Trello, Loom etc. **URLs read (https://atlassian.design/...):** `foundations`, `foundations/{typography,spacing,radius,border,elevation,color,motion,accessibility,iconography,content}`, `foundations/content/{voice-tone,designing-messages,language-and-grammar}`, `get-started/about-atlassian-design-system`, `license`.
**License.** ⚠️ The ADS licence (atlassian.design/license) grants use **only for add-ons that interoperate with Atlassian products**; individual packages may carry OSS licences. Treat guidance as cite-and-paraphrase only; don't copy tokens/assets wholesale.

**Typography.** App fonts Atlassian Sans/Mono (brand font Charlie Sans for marketing only); rem-based tokens. Headings (all bold): XXL 32/36, XL 28/32, L 24/28, M 20/24, S 16/20, XS 14/20, XXS 12/16. Body: L 16/24 (long-form default), **M 14/20 (component default)**, S 12/16; paragraph spacing 16/12/8. Medium weight for text next to line icons; bold sparingly. One h1, no skipped heading levels; use heading styles rather than ad-hoc bold.

**Spacing.** 8px base; tokens named by % of base (`space.100` = 8px): 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80 (+ negatives to −32). Usage bands: 0–8px inside small components; 12–24px larger components/cards; 32–80px layout. Group by similarity (equal spacing for similar items) and proximity.

**Radius and border.** `radius.xsmall` 2 (badges, checkboxes), small 4 (labels, tags, tooltips, compact buttons), **medium 6 (buttons, inputs, nav items)**, large 8 (cards, menus, floating UI), xlarge 12 (modals, large containers, tables), xxlarge 16 (video), full (people-related UI). **Focus ring: 2px offset, radius = component radius + 2px** (the concentric rule again), width 2px; selected border 2px; default border 1px; subtle accent backgrounds need an accent border to reach 3:1.

**Elevation.** Four levels — sunken (wells like Kanban columns; only on default surface), default, raised (only for movable cards; if used for emphasis, one focal point per screen), overlay (modals, menus, floating toolbars) + overflow shadow for scroll cues. Surface + shadow tokens must be paired; in dark mode higher = lighter surface. Use **either** surface-colour hover/pressed tokens **or** elevation transitions, not both; no more than one floating button side by side; check overlay contrast separately in dark mode. Z-index scale: nav 200, inline dialog 300, popup 400, blanket 500, modal 510, flag 600, spotlight 700, tooltip 800.

**Colour.** Token grammar `color.<property>.<role>.<emphasis>.<state>`. Roles: neutral, brand, information, success, warning, danger, discovery, accent (meaningless, swappable), inverse, input. **Never use an accent colour where the colour carries meaning.** Emphasis from subtlest to boldest; inverse tokens on bold backgrounds (special `warning.inverse` for yellow). Contrast: text <24px 4.5:1; essential UI and text ≥24px 3:1.

**Motion (early access, 2026).** Principles: human, clarity (a clarifying layer rather than decoration), accessible, performant. **Interactions 50–150ms; transitions 150–400ms** (dropdown 150, modal 250). Curves: ease-out bold `cubic-bezier(0,0.4,0,1)` (entrances like panels/flags), ease-in-out bold `(0.4,0,0,1)` (modal scale, repositioning), ease-in practical `(0.6,0,0.8,0.6)` (exits), ease-out practical `(0.4,1,0.6,1)` (popups, hover fades). Decision lenses: match motion to function and size; **"if I remove this, does the user lose information or context?"**; never make users wait; **anything triggered dozens of times a day stays under 150ms**; exits faster than entrances; one focal point, no competing simultaneous animations; reduced motion = motion off and instant; no flashing, rapid oscillation or large sweeping motion; brand expression through restraint and polish rather than spectacle.

**Content.** Voice: bold, optimistic, practical *with a wink*; tone dial depends on the user's state (less bold/optimistic for new, confused or blocked users; the wink only in success/social moments). Delight means small flourishes rather than jokes or cheekiness, and only after trust is built. Mechanics: sentence case everywhere; no articles in buttons/labels ("Create password"); no gerunds in UI headings; headings as statements with action verbs, no periods; lists ≤6 items; avoid e.g./i.e./etc./&; contractions encouraged; "they" for unknown gender. Reading level ages 12–14. Message taxonomy (information, error, success, warning, feature discovery) mapped to components (empty state, banner, flag, section message, inline message, modal, spotlight); banners only for critical system-level messages.

**Strengths.** Very operational motion guidance (frequency-based duration budget), explicit elevation discipline, radius-to-component mapping, focus-ring geometry.
**Weaknesses.** Licence-restricted; motion system still early access; many details behind internal links.
**Adopt.** Motion frequency budget (<150ms for frequent interactions) and the removal test; exit-faster-than-entrance; one animated focal point; elevation pairing/one-focal-raised rule; focus ring offset/radius rule; "accent ≠ semantic" colour rule; tone dial by user state; lists ≤6.


### 2.8 GitHub Primer

**What it is.** GitHub's product design system (React + Rails ViewComponents, tokens in `primer/primitives`). **URLs read (https://primer.style/product/...):** `getting-started/foundations/{layout,typography,color-usage,icons,content,responsive}`, `primitives/{typography,color,size}`, `ui-patterns/{loading,empty-states,forms,degraded-experiences,saving,notification-messaging}`.
**License.** primer/primitives, primer/css, @primer/react **MIT**; Mona Sans **OFL-1.1** (github/mona-sans).

**Layout and responsiveness.** Viewport ranges: **narrow <768px (1 column), regular ≥768px (≤2 columns), wide ≥1400px (≤3 columns)**; ruler breakpoints 320/544/768/1012/1280/1400; content padding 16px (24px at ≥1280); page max-width 1280px so content paragraphs never get too many words per line; interstitial pages (sign-in, long operations) max 320px wide. App header scrolls with the page (never fixed); context region shows owner/repo, not a full breadcrumb; split-page panes flush left (a right-flushed pane's scrollbar would collide with the page's). Narrow adaptations: split list/detail into pages, pane → bottom sheet, or stack (but don't stack a link-heavy pane above content).
**Input modality.** Pointer media queries (`pointer: coarse/fine`) are called **unreliable** — don't gate UI on them; don't hide information behind hover on devices without hover.
**Targets.** AA minimum **24px** (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8), aim for **44px** (AAA) on mobile — use the large (40px) or xlarge (48px) control rather than enlarging a 32px button's hit area invisibly. Control heights: xsmall 24, small 28, medium 32, large 40, xlarge 48.

**Typography.** Stack now starts with **Mona Sans VF**, then system fonts and Noto Sans; rem sizes, unitless line heights on a 4px grid. Display 40px/1.375 w500; Title L 32/1.5 w600; Title M 20/1.625 w600; Title S 16/1.5 w600; Subtitle 20/1.625 w400; Body L 16/1.5; **Body M 14/1.5**; Body S 12/1.625; Caption 12/1.25; code 13/1.5. Lines ≈80 characters or fewer; left-aligned ragged-right; no colour as primary emphasis; don't reorder heading tags for visual effect; use weight tokens, not arbitrary numbers.

**Colour.** Base → functional → component tokens; base tokens never used directly. Neutral scale steps 0–13 (light and dark scales are inverted so functional tokens can be shared): 0–6 backgrounds, 7–8 borders (**step 8 = minimum for interactive control borders**), 9–10 text (step 9 ≥ minimum on 0–4; step 10 on 5–6). **Contrast is computed against `bgColor-muted`** (the harder case) so both muted and default backgrounds pass. High-contrast themes target **7:1** for most text and interactive elements. Nine themes incl. colour-blind variants. Semantic roles: accent (links/selection/focus), success (**primary buttons are green**), attention, danger, open/closed/done (workflow states), sponsors; muted vs emphasis variants; emphasis backgrounds always paired with `fgColor-onEmphasis`. Radius tokens small 3 / medium 6 (default) / large 12 / full; borders 1/2/4px; focus outline 2px.

**Patterns.**
- Loading thresholds: **<1s show nothing; 1–3s indeterminate; 3–10s determinate if possible; >10s determinate and move to a background task**. Show items incrementally; prioritise the most important data; one loader for a cluster (one announcement for screen readers); place the indicator where the content will appear and avoid layout shift; announce status with `role="status"`/`aria-live`, use `aria-busy` while updating; move focus to new content or the first error; keep loading animation subtle (don't remove it for reduced-motion users).
- Saving: start with **explicit save**; **never mix auto-save and explicit save in one form**; one save button per page; **don't disable or hide the save button even if the form is invalid or unchanged** (disabled buttons can't be tabbed to and are low-contrast); declarative controls (checkbox groups, selects in forms) → explicit save; imperative controls (toggles) → auto-save; preserve data on save errors; button order: submit then cancel (bottom-left), cancel then submit in dialogs.
- Degraded experiences: don't hide that something is wrong; render degraded content if the main experience still works; remove non-functional buttons unless they're core to the workflow (then explain instead of disabling).
- Empty states (Blankslate): graphic, primary text, optional secondary text, one primary action; error empty states use an alert icon, not playful art; first-run may be playful; explain the feature, not just the absence.
**Content.** Grade 7 or below; "don't sound like a robot"; remove unnecessary adjectives and adverbs; active voice; sentence case; no "here/click here"; humour unwelcome in errors, waits and failures; **don't apologise** (state the failure plainly); no "Oops"; feedback reuses the trigger's verb (if the button says transfer, the status says transfer); labels/buttons without punctuation, starting with an imperative verb; avoid emojis (if any, end of sentence, never repeated); relative time for recent events, precise dates for deadlines/expiry.

**Strengths.** Concrete wait-time thresholds; save-pattern rules; contrast-against-the-harder-background rule; candid stance on unreliable pointer queries; content rules that directly counter chatty AI copy.
**Weaknesses.** GitHub-specific (green primary is idiosyncratic); some docs internal-only.
**Adopt.** Loading thresholds; save-pattern rules; no disabled submit; contrast checked against the muted background; 24px minimum / 44px target; one save button; no-apology/no-oops; feedback reuses trigger verb; incremental loading with no layout shift.


### 2.9 Radix Colors and Radix Themes

**What it is.** Open-source colour system (Radix Colors) and themeable React component library (Radix Themes) — the colour/token foundation under shadcn/ui-style stacks that many AI tools emit. **URLs read (https://www.radix-ui.com/...):** `colors/docs/palette-composition/{understanding-the-scale,composing-a-palette}`, `colors/docs/overview/{aliasing,installation}`, `themes/docs/theme/{overview,color,typography,spacing,radius,shadows,breakpoints,dark-mode}`.
**License.** radix-ui/colors and radix-ui/themes **MIT**.

**Colour scale semantics (the most reusable idea).** Every scale has 12 steps, each with a job: **1 app background, 2 subtle background, 3 component background, 4 hovered component, 5 active/selected component, 6 subtle non-interactive borders/separators, 7 interactive borders and focus rings, 8 hovered/strong borders and focus rings, 9 solid background (purest chroma), 10 hovered solid, 11 low-contrast text, 12 high-contrast text.** Steps 11 and 12 are **guaranteed APCA Lc 60 and Lc 90 on step 2** of the same scale. Each colour ships light, dark and alpha (and P3) variants; alpha variants look identical over the page background and adapt over other surfaces. Most step-9 colours take white text; sky, mint, lime, yellow, amber take dark text.
- Palette composition: one accent + one gray; six grays (pure gray, mauve, slate, sage, olive, sand); **"natural pairing" = gray tinted toward the accent's hue** (e.g., slate with blue/indigo, sage with green/teal, sand with amber/orange) for harmony; neutral gray for neutrality; beware saturated grays as dark-mode backgrounds behind colourful badges. Semantic suggestions: error red/ruby/tomato/crimson; success green/teal/jade/grass/mint; warning yellow/amber/orange; info blue/indigo/sky/cyan. Use the gray scale for app text (functional vibe) and the accent scale for text on marketing pages (colourful vibe). Scales "are not intended to be customised" — add custom brand scales alongside.
**Themes numbers.** Type steps 1–9: 12/16, 14/20, 16/24, 18/26, 20/28, 24/30, 28/36, 35/40, 60/60 px, with letter-spacing tightening from +0.0025em to **−0.025em at 60px**. Space 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64; global `scaling` 90–110 % for density. Radius is a theme *factor* (none/small/medium/large/full) applied contextually — e.g., checkboxes never become fully round so they can't be mistaken for radios. Shadows 1 (inset) to 6 (dialogs). Breakpoints 520 / 768 / 1024 / 1280 / 1640 (min-width). Focus colour auto-matched to the component accent (`--focus-8`). Dark mode via class switching (avoid flash on hydration).

**Strengths.** Step-to-job mapping makes colour usage checkable in code (is text on steps 11–12? are borders on 6–8?); APCA-based guarantees; tinted-gray pairing is a cheap way to look less generic.
**Weaknesses.** Default accent (indigo) and translucent panels contribute to a recognisable "shadcn/Radix default" look; APCA guarantees are not WCAG 2.x guarantees.
**Adopt.** 12-step role mapping as a lint rule; tinted-gray pairing; alpha colours for overlays; radius never erases semantic shape differences (checkbox vs radio).

### 2.10 Tailwind CSS defaults — the "default look" AI UIs inherit

**What it is.** The utility-first CSS framework most AI code generators emit. **Read:** `packages/tailwindcss/theme.css` and `preflight.css` (main branch; latest release **v4.3.3, 2026-07-16**), CHANGELOG.md, PR #20318; Adam Wathan's post (https://x.com/adamwathan/status/1953510802159219096, Aug 2025).
**License.** MIT.

**Default tokens (v4.3).** Spacing unit `--spacing: 0.25rem` (4px; any multiple allowed). Breakpoints sm 40rem (640), md 48rem (768), lg 64rem (1024), xl 80rem (1280), 2xl 96rem (1536). Type: xs 12/16, sm 14/20, **base 16/24**, lg 18/28, xl 20/28, 2xl 24/32, 3xl 30/36, 4xl 36/40, 5xl 48 (lh 1), 6xl 60, 7xl 72, 8xl 96, 9xl 128 — i.e., **line-height collapses to 1.0 from 48px up** and nothing in the scale is tied to a ratio. Weights 100–900 all exposed; tracking −0.05 to +0.1em; leading 1.25–2. Radius xs 2, sm 4, md 6, lg 8, xl 12, 2xl 16, 3xl 24, 4xl 32px. Shadows: two-layer black at 5–25 % (`shadow-sm` … `shadow-2xl`). Easing `ease-out (0,0,0.2,1)`, `ease-in-out (0.4,0,0.2,1)`; **default transition 150ms**. Colours: 26 palettes × 11 steps (50–950) in OKLCH (2026 added mauve, olive, mist, taupe neutrals). Preflight removes margins, sets line-height 1.5, borders inherit `currentColor` by default, placeholders = semi-transparent current colour.
**CJK-relevant change (v4.3.3).** `--font-sans` now lists explicit platform fonts (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, 'Helvetica Neue', 'Noto Sans', Arial, sans-serif, emoji…`) **instead of `system-ui`/`ui-sans-serif`**, because on Windows `system-ui` ignores the page's `lang` and rendered characters wrongly under the Japanese locale (PR #20318, citing MDN's warning that `system-ui` is for UI chrome, not paragraphs; Astro Starlight and VitePress made the same change).
**Why it matters for "AI slop".** Tailwind's tokens are a palette, not a design: everything is available, nothing is prescribed (no semantic roles, no type ratio, no state model). Generators therefore converge on the most frequent combinations in training data. Adam Wathan's own post jokingly apologised for making every Tailwind UI button `bg-indigo-500`, "leading to every AI generated UI on earth also being indigo"; the training-data mechanism is plausible but **[unverified]**. Recognisable default signature: indigo/violet-500 primary or indigo→purple gradient, gray-50/white page, `rounded-xl/2xl` cards with `shadow-sm/md`, `text-4xl+` headings with line-height 1, uniform `p-6`/`gap-6` everywhere, 150ms ease transitions on everything, and the system font. None of these is wrong individually; together they read as "unconsidered default".
**Adopt.** Use Tailwind's mechanics but require a project token layer (semantic roles, a type ratio, a state model) before generating UI; flag raw palette classes (`bg-indigo-500`) in components; flag line-height 1 on multi-line headings; flag uniform spacing; avoid `system-ui` for CJK paragraph text.


### 2.11 Ant Design (Ant Group) — 设计价值观、字体、布局、文案

**What it is.** The dominant Chinese enterprise ("中后台") design system; React components + token-based theming (v5+). **Read:** markdown sources via `https://ant.design/docs/spec/<page>-cn.md` (the site is client-rendered but publishes `.md` and `llms.txt`): `values`, `font`, `layout`, `colors`, `shadow`, `motion`, `proximity`, `alignment`, `contrast`, `repetition`, `lightweight`, `transition`, `reaction`, `copywriting`, `data-format`, `buttons`, `feedback` (navigation, icon, introduce, overview, visualization-page, illustration, research-overview fetched but not reviewed). Token code: `ant-design/components/theme/themes/{seed.ts, shared/genFontSizes.ts, shared/genSizeMapToken.ts, shared/genControlHeight.ts, shared/genRadius.ts, shared/genCommonMapToken.ts}` and `theme/util/alias.ts` (breakpoints 480/576/768/992/1200/1600/1920; control heights 24/32/40; radius 2/4/6/8).
**License.** antd **MIT**; docs content © Ant Group (paraphrase/cite).

**Design values (设计价值观).** 自然 Natural (lower cognitive cost by borrowing natural laws; proactive help via behaviour analysis/AI), 确定性 Certainty (low "collaboration entropy": restraint — "can do, but decided not to"; object-oriented, modular design; cross-product consistency), 意义感 Meaningfulness (clear goals + immediate feedback; moderate challenge so users stay in flow; Occam's razor — add no unnecessary elements), 生长性 Growth (value discoverability; human–machine co-evolution). Restraint (克制) recurs in every spec page — colour, type sizes, motion.

**Typography (字体).** Font stack: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, 'Noto Sans', sans-serif, emoji…` — **no explicit CJK family; it relies on OS fallback** (PingFang SC on Apple, Microsoft YaHei on Windows). Base size raised from 12 to **14px** based on 50 cm viewing distance; **base line height 22px**. Scale from code: size = 14·e^(i/5) rounded down to even → **12, 14, 16, 20, 24, 30, 38, 46, 56, 68**; **line-height = size + 8px** (ratios 1.67 → 1.12). Use **3–5 sizes per system** (except showcase pages); weights only 400/500 (600 for bold Latin); **text-to-background contrast ≥7:1 (AAA) for body and headings**; `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for numbers compared vertically; fewer styles beat many — don't use many sizes/colours/weights to force emphasis.

**Layout (布局).** 1440px artboard; target resolutions 1920/1440/1366 (some 1280); 8px grid unit; **24-column grid with fixed gutters**; two adaptation modes — side-nav layouts fix the nav and scale the work area; top-nav layouts define minimum side margins, then scale the content. Vertical spacing **y = 8 + 8n** (small/medium/large = 8/16/24). Seed tokens: `sizeUnit 4`, `controlHeight 32`, `borderRadius 6`, `fontSize 14`, primary `#1677ff`.

**Layout principles (CRAP + Fitts).** Proximity (related = closer), alignment (one visual starting edge; **form labels right-aligned on the colon** in dense forms; **numbers right-aligned with equal decimal places**), contrast (contrast must be decisive, never timid; emphasise by strengthening the main item *or* weakening others; **stay neutral and don't nudge when the user must make a careful decision**), repetition. Lightweight interaction: Fitts's law, contextual tools; visible area ≠ clickable area.

**Colour.** HSB model for design; 12 hues × 10 steps = 120 base colours; **primary = 6th step of its ramp** (`blue-6 #1677FF`); neutral palette of 13 greys implemented as **black/white alpha** (works on any background); functional colours (success/warning/error/info) kept constant across a product; colour use is "restrained" — for information, guidance and feedback, not decoration (except illustrations and showcase pages).

**Feedback, buttons, motion, shadow.** At most **one primary button per button area**; one call-to-action per screen; icon-only buttons need a tooltip; dashed button = invitation to add content; ghost buttons only on dark/complex backgrounds. Loading feedback when an operation exceeds **~2 s**, with cancel for long operations; global `message` auto-dismisses (default **3 s**) — **important failures belong in a dialog, not a toast**; inline validation text sits next to the field and doesn't auto-disappear; don't over-feedback simple, visibly successful actions. Motion principles 自然/高效/克制 (natural / efficient / restrained); motion must not drop frames; durations fast 0.1 s / mid 0.2 s / slow 0.3 s (motion unit 0.1 s); curves `easeOutCirc (0.08,0.82,0.17,1)`, `easeInOutCirc (0.78,0.14,0.15,0.86)`, `easeOut (0.215,0.61,0.355,1)`, `easeInOut (0.645,0.045,0.355,1)`. Shadows: four levels (0 inputs, 1 hover lift, 2 dropdowns, 3 dialogs), three-layer shadows since v4, direction by context (up for bottom bars, left/right for drawers and fixed table columns).

**Chinese copywriting (文案) — directly reusable for zh-CN UIs.** User-centred subject; concise; familiar words, no obscure "elegant" diction; consistent terms (action name = destination page title); important info first; precise term choices (其他 not 其它; 登录 not 登陆; 「抱歉」 only when the system is at fault, never for user errors; 验证码 vs 校验码 distinguished). **Address the user as 「你」, not 「您」** (Ant's stance; formal sectors may differ — see §3); never mix 你/我 in one sentence; don't command or blame; avoid absolute statements; avoid exclamation marks except greetings/congratulations; Arabic numerals for statistics; omit terminal punctuation in labels, titles, input hints, tooltips, table cells and buttons; **insert a space between full-width (CJK) and half-width characters ("2 个")** and around inline links; use half-width hyphen in dates (2012-11-12); ellipsis "…".
**Data formats (数据格式).** Thousands separators by default; units lowercase in table headers; amounts right-aligned, "¥123.00" or "CNY123.00"; very large numbers may use 万/亿; dates **yyyy-mm-dd** by default, date ranges with " ～ " (spaces around), 24-hour time `HH:mm:ss`, date and time separated by a space; relative time: <1 min 「刚刚」, <1 h 「N 分钟前」, <24 h 「N 小时前」, otherwise `mm-dd HH:mm`, >1 year `yyyy-mm-dd HH:mm`; masking rules (two-character names 「仲*」; phone numbers keep first 3 + last 4; ID numbers by sensitivity level).

**Strengths.** Mathematically derived, tokenised type scale with a simple line-height rule; the most complete zh-CN copy and data-format rules; strong "restraint" ethic; enterprise-tested.
**Weaknesses.** Dense, enterprise blue look (#1677ff) that reads as "default antd" when un-themed; font stack omits explicit CJK fonts and uses `-apple-system`-first ordering (fine) but Windows CJK depends on OS defaults; colon-right-aligned labels conflict with mobile/accessibility advice elsewhere.
**Adopt.** line-height = size + 8 for 12–20px CJK UI text; 3–5 type sizes; tabular numerals; right-align numbers; ≤1 primary per button group; failures in dialogs not toasts; neutral (no-nudge) styling for consequential choices; zh-CN copy, punctuation and date/number rules; masking patterns.


### 2.12 TDesign (Tencent)

**What it is.** Tencent's cross-framework enterprise design system (Vue/React/mini-program/mobile). The website is a SPA; I read the **source of the guideline pages** from the official repo (shallow clone of https://github.com/Tencent/tdesign): `site/src/locales/pages/{design-basic,design-visual,design-motion-icon}.ts` (bilingual text for values, fonts, dark mode, layout, colour, motion, icons) and `site/src/pages/design/*.vue`.
**License.** **MIT** (repo); Tencent brand assets (Tencent Blue, Tencent Cloud numeric font) are brand-owned — don't reuse as-is.

**Values.** 包容 Inclusive, 多元 Diverse, 进化 Evolving, 连接 Connected. Typography principles: 好用 / 好记 / 美观 (usable, memorable, beautiful).
**Typography — the most explicit CJK reasoning found.**
- Recommended stack: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Roboto, Hiragino Sans GB, Microsoft YaHei UI, Microsoft YaHei, Source Han Sans CN, sans-serif, emoji…`. Warns that an undeclared font is the browser *default*, not the system font (Windows 7 rendered SimSun instead of Microsoft YaHei).
- Desktop: body **14px**, minimum **12px**; mobile minimum/base 10px [as stated; note this is below Apple's 11pt and M3's practical minimums]. Primary scale steps of **+2px**; display scale steps of +4/+8/+12/+16 to avoid many tiny steps.
- **Line height = font size + 8** — explicitly argued: a fixed 1.5× ratio makes large text too loose and breaks continuity in mixed-size layouts; +8 equals 1.5× at 14/16px and keeps the inter-line gap constant (same rule Ant Design derives in code).

- Text colours are black/white with alpha (4 levels: primary, secondary, placeholder, disabled) for light/dark adaptability; contrast ≥4.5:1; dark mode slightly lowers alpha to equalise perceived contrast.
- Tabular (monospaced) figures for table data.
**Dark mode.** Content first (legibility over showiness); **avoid highly saturated colours on dark surfaces (they appear to vibrate)**; keep hierarchy identical across modes; dark palette derived algorithmically from light (CIELab/HSL interpolation); text ≥4.5:1.
**Layout.** Artboard 1440 or 1920; **8px base with extra 4 and 12**; **12-column grid**, gutter 16px, side margin 24px; nav layouts side / top / mixed (side nav for deep hierarchies; top nav for wide content with fixed width); sidebar 232px expanded, 64px collapsed, auto-collapse below **992px**; breakpoints **768 / 992 / 1200** (deliberately below common screen widths to avoid Safari scrollbar-induced flips); minimum page width 768px (fixed grid below, fluid above).
**Colour.** Brand "Tencent Blue" + 4 functional colours (each a 10-step ramp built in **HCT** with interpolated curves for even lightness across hues) + 14 neutrals (CIELab) + optional **brand-tinted neutrals mixing 8–12 % of the brand colour** + 8 extended hues for data-viz (qualitative/sequential palettes validated with CIEDE2000 and contrast). Global semantic tokens → component tokens. Reported contrasts: brand blue-7 6.54:1, red-6 4.32:1, orange-5 3.12:1, green-5 3.16:1 — i.e., **TDesign's own warning/success colours are not text-safe at 4.5:1** (fine for icons/fills ≥3:1).
**Motion.** Principles 理解 / 聚焦 / 共情 (understand, focus, empathise); not everything needs to move — motion should aid perception, not be mandatory. Macro patterns: **axis motion** (spatial relationships; X for horizontal tabs, Y for vertical, Z for dialogs/popovers), **container transform** (shared content), **fade** (no spatial/logical link). Durations: **desktop 200–600ms, mobile 100–400ms; desktop = mobile × 2** (argued from viewing distance; 100ms ≈ perception onset, 1000ms ≈ limit). Cubic easing by default; elastic only for cartoon/illustration; linear only for fades concurrent with other motion; curved (not straight) paths for two-axis moves; **no staggered entrance on first page load in B2B — show the page or fade it**. A downloadable **motion self-check**: what problem does it solve; does it reduce understanding cost without adding entropy; is it perceivable on every device; is the curve right; is it plain enough ("if B2B motion is too eye-catching, weaken it"); is it over-choreographed; **can the information be conveyed statically if the motion is removed?**
**Icons.** 24×24 drawing grid (20×20 live area) delivered at 16px; 2px stroke; integer coordinates, pixel-aligned; angles at 45° or multiples of 15°; principles simple / precise / moderate.

**Strengths.** Evidence-style reasoning for CJK-specific decisions (+8 line height, 400/600 weights on Windows, explicit CJK font stack, SimSun trap); motion self-check; HCT-based ramps.
**Weaknesses.** Some claims are internal ("research shows") without citations; 10px mobile minimum is aggressive; desktop-longer-than-mobile durations contradict common practice.
**Adopt.** Explicit CJK font stack; line-height = size + 8 for UI text; use 400/600 (not 500) for CJK weight contrast; desaturate accents in dark mode; motion self-check questions; no staggered first-load in productive apps; brand-tinted neutrals at 8–12 %.


### 2.13 Arco Design (ByteDance)

**What it is.** ByteDance's enterprise design system (React/Vue). arco.design is a JS shell; the spec text lives in the official repo **arco-design/arco-doc-site** (`docs_spec/{values-of-arcodesign,philosophy,style-guideline,introduce}.zh-CN.md` + component specs), which I cloned and read; I also read the official agent skill **arco-design/arco-design-skill** (`skills/arco-design/SKILL.md`, MIT, updated 2026-06) — it is an API reference for agents, not design-quality guidance.

**Values and principles.** 清晰 clarity, 一致 consistency, 韵律 rhythm, 开放 openness — "pragmatic romanticism" (清晰/一致 = pragmatic, 韵律/开放 = romantic). Principles are essentially Nielsen's heuristics restated: timely feedback, match the real world, consistency, error prevention, recognition over recall, **highlight the key point (people scan)**, helpful error messages (words, not "404"), and a **help hierarchy: best = no hint needed → one-time hint → persistent hint → documentation**.
**Style guideline (numbers).** Primary `#165DFF`; 13 generated base palettes; mask `#1D2129` at **60 %**. ### 2.15 W3C "Requirements for Chinese Text Layout" (clreq) + CSS Text 4 + WCAG CJK clauses

**What it is.** W3C Group Note (trilingual en/zh-Hans/zh-Hant) by the Chinese Layout Task Force; the version read is the **Group Note Draft of 1 September 2026** (https://www.w3.org/TR/clreq/). I extracted the English text of §1.2, §3.1.1, §5.3, §5.4.1, §6.1.1–6.1.3, §6.2 (first-line indents, alignment), §6.3 (spacing principles, punctuation width adjustment, mixed text in horizontal mode), §6.4, §7.1 (type area, orphans/widows). Supplemented with CSS Text Level 4 Editor's Draft (`text-autospace`, https://drafts.csswg.org/css-text-4/), MDN (`text-autospace` Baseline 2025 since Nov 2025; `text-spacing-trim` not Baseline), and WCAG 2.2 Understanding SC 1.4.8 (https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/visual-presentation.html).
**License.** W3C Document License — quote sparingly, cite; ideas freely usable.

**Facts and rules (evaluable).**
- **Regions, not scripts:** layout rules differ by region (Mainland, Taiwan, Hong Kong…), so user agents should select rules by region rather than "Simplified vs Traditional" (§1.2). Practical consequence: set `lang="zh-CN"` / `zh-TW` / `zh-HK` correctly — it selects glyph variants, punctuation positions (Mainland punctuation sits at the character-frame start; TW/HK punctuation is centred) and line-breaking behaviour.
- All Han characters and CJK punctuation occupy square 1:1 frames; text is **set solid** (no inter-character spacing) by default; loosened spacing only for short running heads, captions, poetry and children's material; reduced spacing only for magazine/ad headlines as an effect (§6.3.1). ⇒ **Don't apply the Latin habit of negative letter-spacing to CJK body or UI text.**
- Four core typeface styles: **Song/Ming** (body text, headings in bold), **Kai** (textbooks, official docs in Taiwan, quotations), **Hei** (sans; headings/signage, increasingly body on screens; thicker Hei indicates emphasis), **Fangsong** (Mainland government documents, quotations) (§3.1.1). Emphasis traditionally uses emphasis dots under the characters (●/•) or a heavier/different typeface — not italics (§5.3).
- **Ellipsis = six dots occupying two character widths** (two U+2026, or U+22EF per Unicode guidance), never split across lines (§5.4.1, citing GB/T 15834—2011). ⇒ Ant Design/Arco's "half-width single …" is a UI convention that departs from the national standard; acceptable for programmatic truncation, not for prose.
- **Line-breaking prohibitions** (recommended "basic" set): pause/stop punctuation (、，；：。！？), closing quotes/brackets, connector marks and interpuncts must not start a line; opening quotes/brackets must not end a line; GB style additionally keeps two-em dashes and ellipses off the line start (§6.1.1). Never break: two-em dash/ellipsis units, numerals, numerals with %/‰/°/℃, sign + numeral, currency + numeral, superscripts/annotation marks with their base (§6.1.2). Hanging punctuation is uncommon in Chinese (§6.1.3).
- **Punctuation width:** adjacent full-width punctuation (2 em) compresses to 1.5 em (or 1 em in some styles); full-width punctuation at line end shall be trimmed to half width (GB/T 15834 §5.1.10) (§6.3.2). CSS: `text-spacing-trim` (initial `normal`) does this but is **not Baseline** (Chromium-only) and needs fonts with `halt`/`chws` features.
- **Mixed Han–Latin spacing:** up to **¼ em** between a Han character and an adjacent Western letter or European numeral (not at line ends, not next to Chinese commas/full stops or inside Chinese brackets); alternatively a normal U+0020 space (§6.3.3). CSS `text-autospace` (initial `normal` = ideograph-alpha + ideograph-numeric) inserts **⅛ ic** automatically when no space is typed; the spec notes conventions range from ¼ ic (historical) to ⅛ ic (proportional); **Baseline since Nov 2025**. Typed spaces suppress autospace (it only applies to directly adjoining characters).
- **Line length and leading:** books use 17–40 characters per line; body text should be **≥10 and ≤48 characters per line in horizontal mode** (≤55 vertical); line length should be an integer multiple of the font size so justified lines end flush; line gap commonly **50–100 % of the character frame** (i.e., CSS line-height ≈1.5–2.0) and constant throughout (§7.1). WCAG 1.4.8 (AAA) caps blocks at **80 characters or 40 CJK glyphs**, requires leading ≥1.5 and paragraph spacing ≥1.5× the line spacing, and no full justification — but exempts presentation aspects a writing system doesn't use.
- **Minimum size:** print body is size 5 (10.5 pt); the acceptable minimum for content is size 6 (≈7.875 pt) because complex Han structures become illegible below it (§7.1). On screens the systems converge on **12px minimum, 14px UI body** (Ant, Arco, TDesign, Semi).
- **Paragraphs:** books use a 2-em first-line indent and no extra paragraph spacing; the alternative (common on the web) is no indent + paragraph spacing — don't combine both (§6.2.1). **Orphans:** avoid a last line with a single character (or character + punctuation) (§7.1.2).

### 3.2 Consensus (what nearly everyone agrees on)
1. **Tokens, roles and restraint.** Every mature system routes colour, type, spacing, radius, elevation and motion through semantic tokens, and each one independently preaches restraint: Apple (accent judiciously; delight is not decoration), Ant (克制 restraint), Carbon (expressive only for occasional moments), Atlassian (brand expression through restraint and polish, not spectacle), GOV.UK (functional copy should go unnoticed), M3 (≤2 hero moments), TDesign (B2B motion kept plain).
2. **Hierarchy through layout, grouping and contrast, not decoration** (Apple WWDC25/26, M3 grids-spacing, Carbon spacing, Atlassian elevation, Ant proximity/contrast).
3. **One primary action per context** (Apple 1–2 prominent per view; GOV.UK one default button; Polaris one per card, none in tables; Ant one per button area; Carbon tertiary for multiple empty states).
4. **Accessibility floor = WCAG AA**, with multiple systems aiming higher for body text (Apple 7:1 aspiration, Ant 7:1, MCU 7:1 discretionary, Primer HC 7:1).
5. **Targets 44–48 for touch, ≥24 absolute**; spacing between targets matters as much as size.
6. **Motion is functional**: short, interruptible, exits faster than entrances, honours reduced motion; frequent interactions near-instant.
7. **Dark mode is a designed theme**, not an inversion; elevated surfaces lighter; re-check contrast.
8. **Copy**: plain, specific, verb-first, consistent terms, no blame, quiet success, errors with a fix.
9. **CJK specifics** (where covered): 14px body with ~1.5–1.6 line height (size + 8), 12px minimum, solid setting, explicit CJK font stack, regional `lang`.

### 3.4 Gaps (what no system gives us)
- **No operational definition of "distinctive" or "not templated".** Systems warn against decoration (Apple, Atlassian, GOV.UK) and cap expressive moments (M3, Carbon), but none offers a measurable check for "this looks like a default template". The skill must add its own heuristics (raw palette classes, single accent, uniform spacing, default radius/shadow combos, default fonts) — these are **our proposals, not industry rules**.
- **Little published evidence.** Only M3 Expressive cites a study programme (vendor-reported, no data); GOV.UK refers to research but rarely publishes numbers; most numeric choices (e.g., +8px line height, 150ms) are craft conventions.
- **Motion accessibility thresholds** are qualitative (except WCAG 2.3 flash limits and Apple's visionOS ~0.2 Hz oscillation warning).
- **CJK** is absent from Western systems except M3's language-height tokens, GOV.UK's generic i18n, and Tailwind's font-stack fix; Japanese/Korean specifics (jlreq, klreq) were out of scope here.
- **Disabled-state legibility floor**, **dark-mode contrast across elevations** and **data-visualisation colour** are only lightly covered (TDesign mentions CIEDE2000 validation; Atlassian warns to re-check overlays in dark mode).

### 3.5 Proposed unified, platform-adaptive web baseline (summary)
- **Type:** content/marketing body 16px (Latin) / 16px (CJK reading); productive UI body 14px (Latin LH 1.43–1.5; CJK LH size + 8); min 12px; 3–5 sizes per screen; ratio ≈1.125–1.25 with ≥2px steps; weights 400 + 600/700 (500 only for Latin-only text); no negative tracking on CJK; tracking for Latin display text per font metrics; tabular numerals in data.
- **Space:** 4px base, scale 2·4·8·12·16·24·32·40·48·64·80; spacing expresses grouping; padding/gap over margins.
- **Layout:** width-based breakpoints at ~600/840/1200/1600 (M3) or the project's framework values, with single pane <600, two panes ≥840, three ≥1600; content ≤1280px; prose ≤75 Latin / ≤40 CJK characters; reflow at 320px.
- **Targets:** ≥24px everywhere, ≥44px for touch-primary controls (48 recommended), ≥8px apart.
- **Colour:** role tokens + 10–12-step ramps (Radix/MCU logic), accent for primary/selection/focus only, semantic colours only for meaning, AA gates + 7:1 aspiration for body text, light/dark/high-contrast verified.
- **Shape/elevation:** size-scaled radii, concentric nesting, flat/tonal surfaces with ≤4 elevation levels, shadows only for floating layers, scrim 32–60 % for modals.
- **States:** rest/hover/focus-visible/pressed/disabled/selected/loading; two cues per state; focus = 2–3px outline with offset, ≥3:1, distinct from hover.
- **Motion:** 70–150ms micro, 150–300ms components, ≤500ms large; decelerate in, accelerate out, exits shorter; springs only for hero moments; full reduced-motion path.
- **Content:** sentence case, verb-first, grade-7 plain language, no hype vocabulary, no blame/apology for user errors, quiet success, errors with fixes; zh-CN rules per §2.11–2.15.
