# Taking part in a study of [product]

[Organisation] is improving [product], [one line: what it is, e.g. "an app for practising vocabulary"]. We would like you to try it while we watch and listen. **We are testing the product, not you.** If something is hard, that is useful for us to know.

## What will happen

- The session takes about [45] minutes, [in person at place / by video call].
- We will ask you to do a few everyday things with [product] and to say what you are thinking as you go.
- After each task we will ask how easy it was, and at the end a few short questions.
- [One or two] colleagues may watch quietly, to take notes.

## Recording (optional)

With your permission we will record [the screen and your voice; we do not need your camera]. The recording is only for the team to check what happened. You can take part without being recorded.

## How we use what we learn

- Notes use a code such as P03 instead of your name.
- We use what we learn only to improve [product]. We do not use it for marketing and we do not sell it.
- Notes without your name may be analysed with software, including AI tools that [Organisation] has approved.
- If we quote what you said in a report, we remove anything that could identify you or anyone you mention. We will only quote you if you agree below.

## How long we keep it

- Recordings: deleted [n] days after the study ends.
- Notes with codes: kept for up to [period], then deleted.
- You can ask us to delete your recording and notes until [date]. After that, they cannot be linked to you.

## Your choices

- Taking part is your choice.
- You can skip any task or question, take a break, or stop at any time without giving a reason.
- Stopping early does not change your thank-you.

## Thank-you

You will receive [amount or voucher] for taking part, paid [when and how]. Everyone receives the same, whatever happens in the session.

## Questions or concerns

Contact [name or role] at [email or phone]. [If an ethics committee or data protection officer applies: you can also contact them at …].
